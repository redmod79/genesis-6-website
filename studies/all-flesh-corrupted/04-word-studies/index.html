    Word Studies: "All Flesh Had Corrupted His Way" (Genesis 6:12)¶

    Hebrew Terms¶

    בָּשָׂר (basar) - "Flesh"¶

    Strong's H1320

    • Transliteration: basar
    • Pronunciation: baw-sawr'
    • Part of Speech: Masculine noun
    • Etymology: From H1319, meaning "flesh (from its freshness)"

    Definition: Flesh - the physical tissue of bodies, the body itself, or figuratively kinship relations. Also used for humanity in its mortal, earthly nature.

    KJV Translation Frequency: - "flesh" - 256 occurrences - "body" - 2 occurrences - "kin" - 2 occurrences - Total: 269 times

    Genesis 6:3 Usage:

    "My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also [is] flesh [בָּשָׂר]"

    Significance: God identifies man as "flesh" - mortal, earthly, contrasted with God's Spirit. This is about human nature, not genetics.

    Genesis 6:12 Usage:

    "all flesh [בָּשָׂר] had corrupted his way"

    Significance: "All flesh" means all humanity (or all creatures). The phrase describes WHO was corrupt, not HOW they became corrupt.

    "All Flesh" Throughout Scripture:

    Reference Context Meaning
    Genesis 6:17 Flood destruction All creatures
    Genesis 7:21 "All flesh died" All creatures
    Psalm 65:2 "shall all flesh come" All people
    Isaiah 40:5 "all flesh shall see" All people
    Isaiah 40:6 "All flesh is grass" Human frailty
    Luke 3:6 "all flesh shall see" All people
    Acts 2:17 "upon all flesh" All people

    Key observation: "All flesh" never refers to genetics or DNA in Scripture. It means either "all creatures" or "all humanity."

    שָׁחַת (shachath) - "Corrupt"¶

    Strong's H7843

    • Transliteration: shachath
    • Pronunciation: shaw-khath'
    • Part of Speech: Verb
    • Etymology: A primitive root meaning "to decay" or causatively "to ruin"

    Definition: To decay, ruin, spoil; morally: to pervert, corrupt, deal corruptly.

    KJV Translation Frequency: - "destroy" - 96 occurrences - "corrupt" - 22 occurrences - "mar" - 7 occurrences - "spoil" - 5 occurrences - Total: 147 times

    Categories of Usage:

    Category Examples Meaning
    Physical destruction Destroy cities, crops, trees Ruin material things
    Moral corruption Corrupt oneself, corrupt ways Ruin character/conduct

    Genesis 6:11-12 Usage:

    v.11: "The earth also was corrupt [נִשְׁחָתָה, nishchatah] before God" v.12: "all flesh had corrupted [הִשְׁחִית, hishchith] his way"

    Key Parallel Passages (All MORAL):

    Passage Text Context
    Exodus 32:7 "thy people...have corrupted [שִׁחֵת] themselves" Golden calf idolatry
    Deuteronomy 4:16 "lest ye corrupt [תַּשְׁחִתוּן] yourselves, and make...a graven image" Warning against idolatry
    Deuteronomy 4:25 "shall corrupt [וְהִשְׁחַתֶּם] yourselves, and make a graven image" Idolatry warning
    Deuteronomy 9:12 "thy people...have corrupted [שִׁחֵת] themselves" Golden calf
    Deuteronomy 31:29 "ye will utterly corrupt [הַשְׁחֵת תַּשְׁחִתוּן] yourselves" Future apostasy
    Deuteronomy 32:5 "They have corrupted [שִׁחֵת] themselves" Perverse generation
    Judges 2:19 "corrupted [יַשְׁחִיתוּ] themselves more than their fathers" Religious apostasy
    Hosea 9:9 "They have deeply corrupted [הֶעְמִיקוּ־שִׁחֵתוּ] themselves" Like Gibeah's depravity

    Critical Observation: In every instance where shachath describes people corrupting THEMSELVES, it refers to MORAL corruption - idolatry, apostasy, covenant violation. Never genetics.

    דֶּרֶךְ (derek) - "Way"¶

    Strong's H1870

    • Transliteration: derek
    • Pronunciation: deh'-rek
    • Part of Speech: Masculine noun
    • Etymology: From H1869 (darak), meaning "to tread"

    Definition: A road (as trodden); figuratively: a course of life, mode of action, manner, conduct.

    KJV Translation Frequency: - "way" - 590 occurrences - "toward" - 31 occurrences - "journey" - 23 occurrences - "manner" - 8 occurrences - Total: 705 times

    Figurative Uses (Moral/Behavioral):

    Category Examples Meaning
    Way of God Psalm 18:30; 25:4 God's commands, standards
    Way of righteousness Proverbs 8:20; 12:28 Righteous conduct
    Way of the wicked Psalm 1:6; Proverbs 4:19 Sinful lifestyle
    Way of man Proverbs 16:9; Jeremiah 10:23 Human conduct/choices

    Genesis 6:12 Usage:

    "all flesh had corrupted his way [דַּרְכּוֹ, darko]"

    Meaning: Each person had corrupted "his way" = his manner of life, his conduct, his behavior.

    Key Parallel - "Corrupt + Way":

    Passage Hebrew Context
    Deuteronomy 9:12 turned from "the way" (הַדֶּרֶךְ) Idolatry
    Deuteronomy 31:29 turn from "the way" (הַדֶּרֶךְ) Apostasy
    Judges 2:19 "stubborn way" (דַּרְכָּם) Religious apostasy
    Ezekiel 16:47 "in all thy ways" (בְּכָל־דְּרָכַיִךְ) Moral corruption
    Malachi 2:8 "departed from the way" (הַדֶּרֶךְ) Covenant violation

    Critical Observation: "Corrupt + way" ALWAYS describes moral corruption - never genetic/biological.

    Greek Terms (New Testament Context)¶

    σάρξ (sarx) - "Flesh"¶

    Strong's G4561

    The Greek equivalent used when the NT references "all flesh":

    Luke 3:6: "all flesh [πᾶσα σὰρξ, pasa sarx] shall see the salvation of God"

    Acts 2:17: "I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh [πᾶσαν σάρκα, pasan sarka]"

    1 Peter 1:24: "all flesh [πᾶσα σὰρξ] is as grass"

    Meaning: All people, all humanity. Never genetic.

    Summary: Word Studies and Their Implications¶

    The Complete Phrase Analysis¶

    כָּל־בָּשָׂר הִשְׁחִית אֶת־דַּרְכּוֹ

    (kol-basar hishchith eth-darko)

    Word Hebrew Meaning
    כָּל (kol) "all, every" Universal scope
    בָּשָׂר (basar) "flesh" Humanity
    הִשְׁחִית (hishchith) "had corrupted" Ruined morally
    דַּרְכּוֹ (darko) "his way" His conduct/manner of life

    Translation: "All humanity had ruined their manner of life"

    Evidence from Word Studies¶

    Term Meaning in Context Genetic Meaning?
    בָּשָׂר (basar) Humanity, mortal nature Never
    שָׁחַת (shachath) Moral corruption, depravity Never
    דֶּרֶךְ (derek) Conduct, manner of life Never

    The Decisive Finding¶

    Every word in the phrase has established moral/behavioral meaning: - "All flesh" = all humanity - "Corrupted" = ruined morally (as in Deut 9:12; 32:5; Judges 2:19) - "His way" = his manner of life/conduct

    No genetic or biological meaning is supported by any of these terms in any biblical usage.
