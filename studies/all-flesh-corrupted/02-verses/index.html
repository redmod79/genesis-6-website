    Verses: "All Flesh Had Corrupted His Way" (Genesis 6:12)¶

    Primary Text - Genesis 6:11-13¶

    Genesis 6:11 The earth also was corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence.

    Genesis 6:12 And God looked upon the earth, and, behold, it was corrupt; for all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth.

    Genesis 6:13 And God said unto Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me; for the earth is filled with violence through them; and, behold, I will destroy them with the earth.

    Observations: - "Corrupt" (shachath) appears twice in v.11-12 - "His way" (darko) - manner of life, conduct - Violence is emphasized twice (v.11, 13) - "All flesh" is judged for moral corruption

    Context - Genesis 6:1-8¶

    Genesis 6:3 And the LORD said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also [is] flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

    Genesis 6:5 And GOD saw that the wickedness of man [was] great in the earth, and [that] every imagination of the thoughts of his heart [was] only evil continually.

    Genesis 6:6 And it repented the LORD that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart.

    Genesis 6:7 And the LORD said, I will destroy man whom I have created from the face of the earth; both man, and beast, and the creeping thing, and the fowls of the air; for it repenteth me that I have made them.

    Observations: - v.3: "he also is flesh" - mortal, human nature - v.5: God's concern is wickedness, evil thoughts - MORAL issues - v.6-7: God is grieved about MAN He created - No mention of genetics, DNA, or biological contamination

    Parallel Passages: "Corrupt" + "Way"¶

    Deuteronomy 9:12 - Golden Calf¶

    Deuteronomy 9:12 And the LORD said unto me, Arise, get thee down quickly from hence; for thy people which thou hast brought forth out of Egypt have corrupted [themselves]; they are quickly turned aside out of the way which I commanded them; they have made them a molten image.

    Context: Israel's idolatry with the golden calf - clearly MORAL corruption.

    Deuteronomy 31:29 - Moses's Warning¶

    Deuteronomy 31:29 For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt [yourselves], and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and evil will befall you in the latter days; because ye will do evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.

    Context: Predicting future idolatry - clearly MORAL corruption.

    Deuteronomy 32:5 - Song of Moses¶

    Deuteronomy 32:5 They have corrupted themselves, their spot [is] not [the spot] of his children: [they are] a perverse and crooked generation.

    Context: Israel's moral failure - "perverse and crooked" describes CHARACTER, not genetics.

    Judges 2:19 - Cycle of Apostasy¶

    Judges 2:19 And it came to pass, when the judge was dead, [that] they returned, and corrupted [themselves] more than their fathers, in following other gods to serve them, and to bow down unto them; they ceased not from their own doings, nor from their stubborn way.

    Context: Religious apostasy - following false gods. Clearly MORAL.

    Hosea 9:9 - Gibeah's Corruption¶

    Hosea 9:9 They have deeply corrupted [themselves], as in the days of Gibeah: [therefore] he will remember their iniquity, he will visit their sins.

    Context: Reference to Judges 19-21 (Levite's concubine incident) - MORAL depravity.

    Psalm 14:1 / Psalm 53:1 - Universal Corruption¶

    Psalm 14:1 The fool hath said in his heart, [There is] no God. They are corrupt, they have done abominable works, [there is] none that doeth good.

    Psalm 53:1 The fool hath said in his heart, [There is] no God. Corrupt are they, and have done abominable iniquity: [there is] none that doeth good.

    Context: Human moral depravity - atheism leading to abominable WORKS.

    Ezekiel 16:47 - Jerusalem's Corruption¶

    Ezekiel 16:47 Yet hast thou not walked after their ways, nor done after their abominations: but, as [if that were] a very little [thing], thou wast corrupted more than they in all thy ways.

    Context: Jerusalem's sins compared to Sodom and Samaria - MORAL corruption.

    Malachi 2:8 - Priestly Corruption¶

    Malachi 2:8 But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith the LORD of hosts.

    Context: Corrupt priests - covenant violation, poor teaching. MORAL failure.

    "All Flesh" Usage in Scripture¶

    Genesis - The Flood Narrative¶

    Genesis 6:17 And, behold, I, even I, do bring a flood of waters upon the earth, to destroy all flesh, wherein [is] the breath of life, from under heaven

    Genesis 7:21 And all flesh died that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and every man

    Genesis 9:11 And I will establish my covenant with you; neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood

    Meaning: All living creatures (humans and animals).

    Universal Human Scope¶

    Psalm 65:2 O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.

    Isaiah 40:5 And the glory of the LORD shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see [it] together

    Isaiah 40:6 The voice said, Cry. And he said, What shall I cry? All flesh [is] grass, and all the goodliness thereof [is] as the flower of the field

    Luke 3:6 And all flesh shall see the salvation of God.

    Acts 2:17 I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh

    Meaning: All humanity, all people.

    Human Frailty¶

    Job 34:15 All flesh shall perish together, and man shall turn again unto dust.

    1 Peter 1:24 For all flesh [is] as grass, and all the glory of man as the flower of grass.

    Meaning: Mortal human nature in contrast to God.

    Summary of Verse Evidence¶

    Category Passages Meaning
    "Corrupt + way" Deut 9:12; 31:29; Judg 2:19; Ezek 16:47; Mal 2:8 Always MORAL
    "All flesh" Gen 6-9; Ps 65:2; Isa 40:5-6; Luke 3:6; Acts 2:17 Humanity/creatures
    Genesis 6 context v.3, 5, 6, 7, 11, 13 All MORAL descriptions

    No verse uses these phrases for genetic or biological corruption.
