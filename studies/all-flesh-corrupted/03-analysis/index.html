    Verse Analysis: "All Flesh Had Corrupted His Way" (Genesis 6:12)¶

    The Argument Examined¶

    The Angel View Claim¶

    "'All flesh had corrupted his way' implies more than moral corruption. The phrase suggests genetic/biological contamination through angel-human hybridization. The flood was necessary to purify the corrupted gene pool."

    The Question¶

    Does the phrase "all flesh had corrupted his way" support a genetic/biological interpretation, or does it describe moral corruption?

    Part 1: The Immediate Context¶

    What Genesis 6 Actually Says¶

    Verse Description Type
    6:3 "he also [is] flesh" Human nature (mortal)
    6:5 "wickedness of man was great" Moral
    6:5 "every imagination of thoughts...only evil continually" Moral
    6:6 "grieved him at his heart" Response to moral failure
    6:11 "earth was corrupt before God" Moral (before God = spiritually)
    6:11 "filled with violence" Moral (behavior)
    6:12 "all flesh had corrupted his way" Moral (conduct)
    6:13 "filled with violence through them" Moral (behavior)

    The context is entirely moral. There is no mention of: - Genetics or DNA - Biological contamination - Hybrid offspring - Gene pool corruption

    The Flow of Thought¶

    1. God observes that wickedness is great (v.5)
    2. Every thought is only evil (v.5) - MORAL condition
    3. God is grieved at heart (v.6) - emotional response to rebellion
    4. Earth is corrupt and violent (v.11) - MORAL state
    5. All flesh had corrupted his way (v.12) - MORAL conduct
    6. Earth filled with violence (v.13) - MORAL behavior
    7. Therefore God will destroy (v.13) - judgment for SIN

    Part 2: What "Corrupt His Way" Means¶

    Hebrew Analysis¶

    שָׁחַת דַּרְכּוֹ (shachath darko) = "corrupt his way"

    • שָׁחַת (shachath) - to ruin, spoil, act corruptly
    • דֶּרֶךְ (derek) - way, road, path; figuratively: manner of life, conduct

    The phrase means: "ruined/spoiled his manner of life" = corrupt CONDUCT.

    How Scripture Uses "Corrupt + Way"¶

    Every parallel passage uses this phrase for MORAL corruption:

    Passage Context Type of Corruption
    Deuteronomy 9:12 Golden calf idolatry Moral - idolatry
    Deuteronomy 31:29 Future apostasy Moral - turning from God
    Deuteronomy 32:5 "Perverse and crooked generation" Moral - character
    Judges 2:19 Following false gods Moral - religious apostasy
    Hosea 9:9 "As in the days of Gibeah" Moral - depravity
    Psalm 14:1; 53:1 "Abominable works" Moral - sinful actions
    Ezekiel 16:47 Jerusalem's sins Moral - abominations
    Malachi 2:8 Corrupt priests Moral - covenant violation

    Zero instances of "corrupt way" meaning genetic corruption.

    The Burden of Proof¶

    If "corrupt his way" means genetic corruption in Genesis 6:12, we would expect: 1. At least one other passage using the phrase genetically 2. Context indicating biological meaning 3. Some Hebrew indication of physical/genetic damage

    None of these exist. The genetic interpretation is read INTO the text, not derived FROM it.

    Part 3: What "All Flesh" Means¶

    Biblical Usage Categories¶

    Category Examples Meaning
    All creatures Gen 6:17, 19; 7:15, 21; 9:15-17 Every air-breathing animal
    All humanity Ps 65:2; Isa 40:5; Luke 3:6; Acts 2:17 All people everywhere
    Human frailty Gen 6:3; Isa 40:6; Job 34:15; 1 Pet 1:24 Mortal nature

    Genesis 6:3 - The Defining Statement¶

    "My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also [is] flesh"

    God identifies the problem: man is "flesh" - meaning: - Mortal, earthly nature - Weak, prone to sin - Contrasted with God's Spirit

    This is NOT about genetics but about human nature prone to sin.

    The Scope in Genesis 6:12¶

    "All flesh had corrupted his way" means: - All humanity (possibly including animals as collateral) - Had ruined their manner of life - Universal moral corruption

    The word "all" emphasizes extent, not a special type of corruption.

    Part 4: The Logical Fallacy¶

    The Angel View's Reasoning¶

    1. "All flesh" is mentioned
    2. Flood destroyed "all flesh"
    3. Therefore "all flesh" must have been genetically corrupted
    4. Therefore angels corrupted human genetics

    The Problem¶

    This is circular reasoning: - It assumes genetic corruption to prove genetic corruption - It ignores the explicit moral descriptions in the text - It imports a meaning not found in any parallel passage

    What the Text Actually Says¶

    What God SAW What God SAID Category
    Wickedness great (6:5) "I will destroy man" (6:7) Moral
    Every thought evil (6:5) "End of all flesh is come" (6:13) Moral
    Earth corrupt (6:11) "Filled with violence through them" (6:13) Moral
    Filled with violence (6:11) "I will destroy them" (6:13) Moral

    God explains His own reasons - all are MORAL.

    Part 5: Why Violence Is Emphasized¶

    Double Emphasis¶

    Verse Statement
    6:11 "the earth was filled with violence"
    6:13 "the earth is filled with violence through them"

    Significance¶

    If the concern were genetic corruption: - Why emphasize VIOLENCE twice? - Why not mention genetic damage? - Why focus on behavior rather than biology?

    Violence is a MORAL problem - the result of "every imagination only evil continually" (6:5).

    The Causal Chain¶

    1. Evil thoughts (6:5) →
    2. Corrupt ways (6:12) →
    3. Violence filling the earth (6:11, 13) →
    4. Divine judgment (6:7, 13)

    This is a moral chain, not a genetic one.

    Part 6: "Before God" - The Moral Indicator¶

    Genesis 6:11¶

    "The earth also was corrupt before God"

    Hebrew: לִפְנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים (liphnei ha'elohim) = "before the face of God"

    This phrase emphasizes: - Moral/spiritual offense - God as the standard being violated - Corruption in His sight

    If the corruption were merely biological/genetic: - It would exist regardless of God - It wouldn't need the qualifier "before God" - The emphasis on God's perspective indicates MORAL offense

    Part 7: Connection to Related Studies¶

    flood-judgment-severity Study¶

    That study established: - Every stated reason for the flood is MORAL (wickedness, evil thoughts, violence) - Hebrew words (shachath, tamim, chamas) describe MORAL qualities - The severity matches the UNIVERSALITY of moral corruption, not a special type

    genesis-6-sons-of-god Study¶

    That study established: - "Sons of God" are more likely the Sethite line than angels - The context is an earthly scene of marriage - Moses uses different terminology for angels (malak) elsewhere - Genesis 6:3 calls them "flesh" - human, not angelic

    Part 8: The Counter-Question¶

    If "All Flesh Corrupted" = Genetic Damage¶

    The angel view must explain:

    1. Why does every parallel use of "corrupt way" mean MORAL corruption?

    2. Why does the context emphasize violence (behavior) twice?

    3. Why does God say "filled with violence THROUGH THEM"?

    4. "Through them" indicates THEIR actions caused the violence

    5. Not that something was done TO them (genetic damage)

    6. Why does God focus on "every imagination of the thoughts"?

    7. Thoughts are MORAL, not genetic

    8. Imagination is about choices, not DNA

    9. Why is "corrupt before God" used?

    10. Moral offense before God
    11. Not biological damage

    Summary of Analysis¶

    What "All Flesh Had Corrupted His Way" Means¶

    Word/Phrase Hebrew Meaning
    All flesh כָּל־בָּשָׂר (kol-basar) All humanity
    Had corrupted שָׁחַת (shachath) Ruined, spoiled morally
    His way דַּרְכּוֹ (darko) Manner of life, conduct

    Translation: "All humanity had ruined their manner of life"

    Evidence Summary¶

    Evidence Type Finding
    Hebrew meaning Moral corruption of conduct
    Parallel passages Always moral corruption
    Immediate context All moral descriptions
    Emphasis on violence Behavior, not biology
    "Before God" qualifier Moral offense
    Stated reasons Wickedness, evil thoughts, violence

    The genetic interpretation has no textual support.
