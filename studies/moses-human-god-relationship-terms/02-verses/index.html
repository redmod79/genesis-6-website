    Verses: Moses's Terminology for Humans with Special Relationship to God¶

    Category 1: "Servant of the LORD" / "My Servant"¶

    Servant of the LORD¶

    Deu 34:5 So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD.

    My Servant (God speaking)¶

    Gen 26:24 And the LORD appeared unto him the same night, and said, I am the God of Abraham thy father: fear not, for I am with thee, and will bless thee, and multiply thy seed for my servant Abraham's sake.

    Num 12:7 My servant Moses is not so, who is faithful in all mine house. Num 12:8 With him will I speak mouth to mouth...

    Num 14:24 But my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land...

    My Servants (Israel collectively)¶

    Lev 25:42 For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt...

    Lev 25:55 For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.

    Category 2: "Man of God"¶

    Deu 33:1 And this is the blessing, wherewith Moses the man of God blessed the children of Israel before his death.

    Category 3: "Prophet" / "Prophetess"¶

    Gen 20:7 Now therefore restore the man his wife; for he is a prophet, and he shall pray for thee, and thou shalt live... (Abraham)

    Exo 7:1 And the LORD said unto Moses, See, I have made thee a god to Pharaoh: and Aaron thy brother shall be thy prophet.

    Exo 15:20 And Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel in her hand...

    Num 11:29 And Moses said unto him, Enviest thou for my sake? would God that all the LORD'S people were prophets, and that the LORD would put his spirit upon them!

    Num 12:6 And he said, Hear now my words: If there be a prophet among you, I the LORD will make myself known unto him in a vision, and will speak unto him in a dream.

    Deu 13:1 If there arise among you a prophet, or a dreamer of dreams, and giveth thee a sign or a wonder... Deu 13:3 Thou shalt not hearken unto the words of that prophet... Deu 13:5 And that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death...

    Deu 18:15 The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken. Deu 18:18 I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth... Deu 18:20 But the prophet, which shall presume to speak a word in my name, which I have not commanded him to speak... Deu 18:22 When a prophet speaketh in the name of the LORD, if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that is the thing which the LORD hath not spoken...

    Deu 34:10 And there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face.

    Category 4: "Children of the LORD" / "Sons"¶

    Deu 14:1 Ye are the children of the LORD your God: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.

    "Sons of God" (bene elohim)¶

    Gen 6:2 That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose.

    Gen 6:4 There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.

    Note: These are the only two occurrences of "sons of God" (bene elohim) in the Pentateuch.

    Category 5: "My People" (God speaking of Israel)¶

    Exo 3:7 And the LORD said, I have surely seen the affliction of my people which are in Egypt... Exo 3:10 Come now therefore, and I will send thee unto Pharaoh, that thou mayest bring forth my people the children of Israel out of Egypt.

    Exo 5:1 Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Let my people go, that they may hold a feast unto me in the wilderness.

    Exo 7:4 ...and bring forth mine armies, and my people the children of Israel, out of the land of Egypt... Exo 7:16 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me in the wilderness...

    Exo 8:1 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me. Exo 8:20 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me. Exo 8:21 ...upon thee, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people... but not upon my people... Exo 8:22 ...to the end thou mayest know that I am the LORD in the midst of the earth. And I will put a division between my people and thy people... Exo 8:23 ...a division between my people and thy people...

    Exo 9:1 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me. Exo 9:13 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me. Exo 9:17 As yet exaltest thou thyself against my people, that thou wilt not let them go?

    Exo 10:3 ...Let my people go, that they may serve me. Exo 10:4 Else, if thou refuse to let my people go...

    (Additional occurrences throughout Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers)

    Category 6: "Holy People" / "Holy Nation"¶

    Exo 19:6 And ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.

    Deu 7:6 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God: the LORD thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth.

    Deu 14:2 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God, and the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations that are upon the earth.

    Deu 14:21 ...for thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God.

    Deu 26:19 And to make thee high above all nations which he hath made, in praise, and in name, and in honour; and that thou mayest be an holy people unto the LORD thy God, as he hath spoken.

    Deu 28:9 The LORD shall establish thee an holy people unto himself, as he hath sworn unto thee, if thou shalt keep the commandments of the LORD thy God, and walk in his ways.

    Category 7: "Chosen" People¶

    Deu 7:6 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God: the LORD thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth.

    Deu 14:2 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God, and the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations that are upon the earth.

    Deu 18:5 For the LORD thy God hath chosen him out of all thy tribes, to stand to minister in the name of the LORD, him and his sons for ever. (Levites)

    Category 8: "Peculiar Treasure" / "Peculiar People"¶

    Exo 19:5 Now therefore, if ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people: for all the earth is mine.

    Deu 14:2 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God, and the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations that are upon the earth.

    Deu 26:18 And the LORD hath avouched thee this day to be his peculiar people, as he hath promised thee, and that thou shouldest keep all his commandments.

    Summary: Moses's Vocabulary for Humans in Special Relationship with God¶

    Term Hebrew/Phrase Occurrences
    My people ammi Many (throughout Exodus especially)
    My servant(s) avdi / avadai Abraham, Moses, Caleb, Israel
    Servant of the LORD eved YHWH Moses (Deu 34:5)
    Man of God ish elohim Moses (Deu 33:1)
    Prophet/prophetess navi / neviah Abraham, Aaron, Miriam, general references
    Children of the LORD bene YHWH elohekem Israel (Deu 14:1)
    Sons of God bene elohim Gen 6:2, 6:4 only
    Holy people/nation am kadosh / goy kadosh Israel
    Chosen bachar Israel, Levites
    Peculiar treasure/people segullah Israel
