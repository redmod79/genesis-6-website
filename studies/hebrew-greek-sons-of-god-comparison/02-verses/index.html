    Hebrew "Sons of God" - Genesis 6:2, 4¶

    Genesis 6:2 (Hebrew)¶

    וַיִּרְא֤וּ בְנֵי־הָֽאֱלֹהִים֙ אֶת־בְּנ֣וֹת הָֽאָדָ֔ם כִּ֥י טֹבֹ֖ת הֵ֑נָּה וַיִּקְח֤וּ לָהֶם֙ נָשִׁ֔ים מִכֹּ֖ל אֲשֶׁ֥ר בָּחָֽרוּ

    "And the sons of God (bene ha'elohim) saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose."

    Key phrase: בְנֵי־הָֽאֱלֹהִים֙ = bene ha'elohim = "sons of the God" - בְנֵי (bene) = sons of (construct plural of בֵּן/ben, H1121) - הָֽאֱלֹהִים (ha'elohim) = the God (H430 with article)

    Genesis 6:4 (Hebrew)¶

    הַנְּפִלִ֞ים הָי֣וּ בָאָרֶץ֮ בַּיָּמִ֣ים הָהֵם֒ וְגַ֣ם אַֽחֲרֵי־כֵ֗ן אֲשֶׁ֨ר יָבֹ֜אוּ בְּנֵ֤י הָֽאֱלֹהִים֙ אֶל־בְּנ֣וֹת הָֽאָדָ֔ם וְיָלְד֖וּ לָהֶ֑ם

    "There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God (bene ha'elohim) came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them"

    Hebrew "Sons/Children of God" Applied to HUMANS¶

    Deuteronomy 14:1 (Hebrew)¶

    בָּנִ֣ים אַתֶּ֔ם לַֽיהוָ֖ה אֱלֹהֵיכֶ֑ם לֹ֣א תִתְגֹּֽדְד֗וּ

    "Ye are the children of the LORD your God (banim attem la'YHWH elohekem): ye shall not cut yourselves..."

    Key phrase: בָּנִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם - בָּנִים (banim) = children/sons (plural of בֵּן/ben, H1121) - לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם = to the LORD your God

    Moses calls ISRAEL "sons/children" of God - using the same root word (בֵּן/ben).

    Hosea 1:10 (Hebrew)¶

    וְהָיָ֗ה בִּמְק֞וֹם אֲשֶׁר־יֵאָמֵ֤ר לָהֶם֙ לֹֽא־עַמִּ֣י אַתֶּ֔ם יֵאָמֵ֥ר לָהֶ֖ם בְּנֵ֥י אֵֽל־חָֽי

    "in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living God (bene el-chay)"

    Key phrase: בְּנֵי אֵל־חָי = bene el-chay = "sons of the living God" - בְּנֵי (bene) = sons of (same word as Genesis 6:2) - אֵל־חָי = living God

    This is applied to ISRAEL - humans called "sons of the living God."

    Deuteronomy 32:5-6 (Hebrew)¶

    v.5: שִׁחֵ֥ת ל֛וֹ לֹ֖א בָּנָ֣יו מוּמָ֑ם דּ֥וֹר עִקֵּ֖שׁ וּפְתַלְתֹּֽל "They have corrupted themselves, their spot is not the spot of his children (banayv): they are a perverse and crooked generation."

    v.6: הֲלֹוא־הוּא֙ אָבִ֣יךָ קָּנֶ֔ךָ ה֥וּא עָֽשְׂךָ֖ וַֽיְכֹנְנֶֽךָ "Is not he thy father (avicha) that hath bought thee? hath he not made thee, and established thee?"

    Key terms: - בָּנָיו (banayv) = his children/sons (from בֵּן/ben) - אָבִיךָ (avicha) = thy father

    Moses calls God "father" and Israel "his children" - using בֵּן (ben).

    Isaiah 43:6 (Hebrew)¶

    אֹמַ֤ר לַצָּפוֹן֙ תֵּ֔נִי וּלְתֵימָ֖ן אַל־תִּכְלָ֑אִי הָבִ֤יאִי בָנַי֙ מֵרָח֔וֹק וּבְנוֹתַ֖י מִקְצֵ֥ה הָאָֽרֶץ

    "I will say to the north, Give up; and to the south, Keep not back: bring my sons (banay) from far, and my daughters (benotay) from the ends of the earth"

    Key terms: - בָנַי (banay) = my sons (from בֵּן/ben) - בְנוֹתַי (benotay) = my daughters

    God calls Israel "my sons" and "my daughters."

    NT Greek "Sons of God" Applied to HUMANS¶

    Romans 8:14 (Greek)¶

    ὅσοι γὰρ πνεύματι θεοῦ ἄγονται οὗτοι υἱοὶ θεοῦ εἰσιν

    "For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are sons of God (huioi theou)"

    Key phrase: υἱοὶ θεοῦ = huioi theou = "sons of God" - υἱοὶ (huioi) = sons (nominative plural of υἱός/huios, G5207) - θεοῦ (theou) = of God (genitive of θεός/theos, G2316)

    Romans 8:19 (Greek)¶

    ἡ γὰρ ἀποκαραδοκία τῆς κτίσεως τὴν ἀποκάλυψιν τῶν υἱῶν τοῦ θεοῦ ἀπεκδέχεται

    "For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God (ton huion tou theou)"

    Galatians 3:26 (Greek)¶

    Πάντες γὰρ υἱοὶ θεοῦ ἐστε διὰ τῆς πίστεως ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ

    "For ye are all sons of God (huioi theou) by faith in Christ Jesus"

    John 1:12 (Greek)¶

    ὅσοι δὲ ἔλαβον αὐτόν ἔδωκεν αὐτοῖς ἐξουσίαν τέκνα θεοῦ γενέσθαι

    "But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become children of God (tekna theou)"

    1 John 3:1-2 (Greek)¶

    v.1: ἵνα τέκνα θεοῦ κληθῶμεν "that we should be called children of God (tekna theou)"

    v.2: Ἀγαπητοί νῦν τέκνα θεοῦ ἐσμεν "Beloved, now are we children of God (tekna theou)"

    NT Quoting OT "Sons of God" for Humans¶

    Romans 9:26 quoting Hosea 1:10¶

    OT (Hosea 1:10): בְּנֵי אֵל־חָי = "sons of the living God"

    NT (Romans 9:26): ἐκεῖ κληθήσονται υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος "there shall they be called the sons of the living God (huioi theou zontos)"

    Critical observation: Paul quotes Hosea's Hebrew בְּנֵי (bene) and translates it with Greek υἱοὶ (huioi). This is direct evidence that huios is the translation equivalent of ben.

    2 Corinthians 6:18 echoing OT patterns¶

    καὶ ἔσομαι ὑμῖν εἰς πατέρα καὶ ὑμεῖς ἔσεσθέ μοι εἰς υἱοὺς καὶ θυγατέρας "And I will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters (huious kai thugateras)"

    Echoes Isaiah 43:6: "bring my sons (בָנַי/banay) from far, and my daughters (בְנוֹתַי/benotay)"

    Comparison Table: Hebrew vs Greek¶

    Hebrew Term Greek Equivalent Example
    בֵּן (ben) υἱός (huios) Both mean "son"
    בָּנִים (banim) υἱοί (huioi) Both mean "sons/children"
    בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים (bene ha'elohim) υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ (huioi tou theou) "sons of God"
    בְּנֵי אֵל־חָי (bene el-chay) υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος (huioi theou zontos) "sons of the living God"

    Direct Translation Evidence¶

    OT Passage (Hebrew) NT Citation (Greek) Finding
    Hosea 1:10 - בְּנֵי אֵל־חָי Romans 9:26 - υἱοὶ θεοῦ ζῶντος ben → huios
    Deuteronomy 14:1 - בָּנִים LXX - υἱοί ben → huios
    Genesis 6:2 - בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים LXX - υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ ben → huios

    The LXX and NT consistently translate Hebrew בֵּן (ben) with Greek υἱός (huios).
