    Verses: Identity of the "Sons of God" in Genesis 6 - Comprehensive Analysis¶

    Primary Text - Genesis 6:1-7 (KJV)¶

    Genesis 6:1 And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them,

    Genesis 6:2 That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they [were] fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose.

    Genesis 6:3 And the LORD said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

    Genesis 6:4 There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare [children] to them, the same [became] mighty men which [were] of old, men of renown.

    Genesis 6:5 And GOD saw that the wickedness of man [was] great in the earth, and [that] every imagination of the thoughts of his heart [was] only evil continually.

    Genesis 6:6 And it repented the LORD that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart.

    Genesis 6:7 And the LORD said, I will destroy man whom I have created from the face of the earth; both man, and beast...

    The Context: Genesis 4-5 (The Two Lines)¶

    Cain's Line (Ungodly)¶

    Genesis 4:16-17 And Cain went out from the presence of the LORD... And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch: and he builded a city...

    Genesis 4:23-24 And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah... I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

    Seth's Line (Godly)¶

    Genesis 4:25-26 And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth... And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: then began men to call upon the name of the LORD.

    Genesis 5:24 And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.

    God's Reasons for the Flood - Genesis 6¶

    Verse Reason Category
    6:3 "He also is flesh" Human nature
    6:5 "Wickedness of man was great" Moral
    6:5 "Every imagination... only evil continually" Moral
    6:11 "Earth was corrupt before God" Moral
    6:11 "Earth was filled with violence" Moral
    6:12 "All flesh had corrupted his way" Moral
    6:13 "Earth is filled with violence" Moral

    NOT mentioned: genetics, DNA, angels, supernatural corruption.

    "Sons of God" - All Five OT Occurrences¶

    In Genesis (Disputed)¶

    Genesis 6:2 the sons of God saw the daughters of men... and they took them wives

    Genesis 6:4 when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men...

    In Job (Heavenly Beings)¶

    Job 1:6 the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan came also among them.

    Job 2:1 the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan came also among them to present himself before the LORD.

    Job 38:7 When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy? [at creation]

    Observation: Job's "sons of God" are in heavenly throne-room scenes with Satan. They do NOT marry or reproduce.

    Moses's Angel Terminology¶

    How Moses DOES Refer to Angels¶

    Genesis 16:7 And the angel of the LORD found her by a fountain...

    Genesis 19:1 And there came two angels to Sodom at even...

    Genesis 22:11 And the angel of the LORD called unto him out of heaven...

    Exodus 3:2 And the angel of the LORD appeared unto him in a flame of fire...

    Numbers 22:23 And the ass saw the angel of the LORD standing in the way...

    Key Term: מַלְאָךְ (malak) = angel/messenger - Used 28+ times in Pentateuch

    Observation: Moses consistently uses "malak" for celestial beings, never "bene elohim."

    Jesus on Angels and Marriage¶

    Matthew 22:30 For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.

    Mark 12:25 For when they shall rise from the dead, they neither marry, nor are given in marriage; but are as the angels which are in heaven.

    Luke 20:35-36 But they which shall be accounted worthy to obtain that world, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry, nor are given in marriage: Neither can they die any more: for they are equal unto the angels; and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection.

    Observation: Jesus explicitly teaches angels do not marry. "As the angels" = categorical statement about angelic nature.

    Angels as Spirits by Nature¶

    Hebrews 1:7 And of the angels he saith, Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire.

    Hebrews 1:14 Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?

    2 Peter 2:4-5 - "Angels That Sinned"¶

    2 Peter 2:4 For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast [them] down to hell, and delivered [them] into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment;

    2 Peter 2:5 And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth [person], a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

    Key insight: Revelation 12:7-9 describes angels being "cast down" - same language, and those angels remain active.

    Jude 6-7 - Angels and Sodom¶

    Jude 6 And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.

    Jude 7 Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.

    Key insight: "In like manner" connects surrounding cities to Sodom, not angels to sexual sin. "Strange flesh" = homosexuality (men pursuing men).

    Revelation 12 - The War in Heaven¶

    Revelation 12:7-9 And there was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and the dragon fought and his angels, And prevailed not; neither was their place found any more in heaven. And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan... he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

    Observation: This describes angels "cast down" (same language as 2 Peter 2:4), yet they remain active with "great wrath" (v.12).

    1 Peter 3:18-20 - "Spirits in Prison"¶

    1 Peter 3:18-20 For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit: By which also he went and preached unto the spirits in prison; Which sometime were disobedient, when once the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing...

    Key insight: Christ preached BY the Spirit THROUGH Noah to those who were disobedient DURING Noah's time. The preaching happened while they were alive.

    Psalm 82 - "Ye Are Gods"¶

    Psalm 82:1 God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods.

    Psalm 82:6-7 I have said, Ye [are] gods; and all of you [are] children of the most High. But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes.

    Jesus's Interpretation¶

    John 10:34-35 Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods? If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God came, and the scripture cannot be broken...

    Observation: Jesus identifies "gods" as those who received God's word (humans), not divine beings.

    Deuteronomy 32:8 - Textual Variant¶

    Deuteronomy 32:8 (MT) When the most High divided to the nations their inheritance... he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.

    Deuteronomy 32:8 (DSS) ...according to the number of the sons of God.

    Key insight: Even if DSS is original, the verse describes territorial division - not angels mating with humans.

    The Nephilim - Genesis 6:4¶

    Genesis 6:4 There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men...

    Timing: Nephilim existed BEFORE ("in those days") and AFTER the unions. This is fatal to the hybrid theory.

    Post-Flood "Nephilim" - Human Lineage¶

    Numbers 13:33 And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants...

    Joshua 15:13-14 ...the city of Arba the father of Anak... And Caleb drove thence the three sons of Anak, Sheshai, and Ahiman, and Talmai, the children of Anak.

    Observation: Post-flood "nephilim" (Anakim) have fully traceable human genealogies.

    "All Flesh Corrupted" Parallels¶

    Deuteronomy 9:12 thy people which thou hast brought forth out of Egypt have corrupted [themselves], they are quickly turned aside out of the way which I commanded them...

    Deuteronomy 31:29 For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt [yourselves], and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you...

    Judges 2:19 And it came to pass, when the judge was dead, [that] they returned, and corrupted [themselves] more than their fathers...

    Observation: Every parallel use of "corrupt + way" describes MORAL corruption - idolatry, apostasy. Never genetic.

    Summary of Verse Evidence¶

    Category Key Passages Finding
    Primary text Genesis 6:1-7 Context is marriage; God addresses "man," calls them "flesh"
    Context Genesis 4-5 Godly (Seth) and ungodly (Cain) lines established
    Moses's terminology 28+ passages Moses uses "malak" for angels, never "bene elohim"
    Jesus's teaching Matt 22:30; Mark 12:25; Luke 20:35-36 Angels do not marry
    Angelic nature Hebrews 1:7, 14 Angels are spirits
    2 Peter 2:4 + Revelation 12 "Cast down" angels remain active; Genesis 6 not required
    Jude 6-7 + Genesis 19 "Strange flesh" = homosexuality; connection to Gen 6 assumed
    Nephilim Genesis 6:4; Numbers 13:33 Existed BEFORE unions; post-flood have human lineage
    "Corrupt way" Deut 9:12; 31:29; Judg 2:19 Always MORAL corruption
    Psalm 82 + John 10:34-35 Jesus says "gods" = humans who received God's word
