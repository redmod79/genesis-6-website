    Word Studies: God's People vs Angels Terminology¶

    PART A: Hebrew Terms for God's Human People¶

    H5971 עַם (am) - "People"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration 'am
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 1,869+ occurrences
    Definition "a people (as a congregated unit); specifically, a tribe (as those of Israel); hence troops or attendants; figuratively, a flock"

    Usage: The most common collective term for Israel as God's people. Often in phrases like "my people" (ammi), "people of God" (am elohim), "holy people" (am kadosh).

    H6918 קָדוֹשׁ (qadosh) - "Holy/Saint"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration qadosh
    Part of Speech Adjective
    BLB Count 116 occurrences
    Definition "sacred (ceremonially or morally); (as noun) God (by eminence), an angel, a saint, a sanctuary"

    Critical Note: This term is used for BOTH humans and angels: - Applied to God's people: "holy people" (am qadosh) - Applied to heavenly beings: "holy ones" (qedoshim) in heavenly council contexts

    H1121 בֵּן (ben) - "Son/Child"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration ben
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 4,000+ occurrences
    Definition "a son (as a builder of the family name), in the widest sense"

    Key Usages: - "Sons of Israel" (bene yisrael) - Very common for Israelites - "Children of the LORD your God" (bene YHWH elohekem) - Deut 14:1 - "Sons of God" (bene elohim) - Gen 6:2,4; Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7

    Note: "bene elohim" in Job refers to heavenly beings; in Genesis 6 it's disputed.

    H5650 עֶבֶד (eved) - "Servant"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration eved
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 800 occurrences
    Definition "a servant, bondman"

    Key Usages: - "Servant of the LORD" (eved YHWH) - Moses, David, prophets - "My servant" (avdi) - Abraham, Moses, Caleb, Job, Israel (as a nation) - Isaiah's "Servant Songs" use this term for the Messiah

    Note: This term is applied ONLY to humans in special relationship with God, NEVER to angels.

    PART B: Hebrew Terms for Angels¶

    H4397 מַלְאָךְ (malak) - "Angel/Messenger"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration malak
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 214 occurrences
    Definition "a messenger; specifically, of God, i.e. an angel (also a prophet, priest or teacher)"

    Key Usages: - "Angel of the LORD" (malak YHWH) - Most common term - "Angel of God" (malak elohim) - "His angel" (malako)

    Note: This is the PRIMARY Hebrew term for celestial beings. It can also refer to human messengers.

    H6635 צָבָא (tsaba) - "Host"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration tsaba
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 485 occurrences
    Definition "a mass of persons (or figuratively, things), especially organized for war (an army); worship"

    Key Usages: - "Host of heaven" (tsaba hashamayim) - Angels or celestial bodies - "LORD of hosts" (YHWH tsabaot) - A divine title - Also used for earthly armies

    Note: Context determines whether it refers to angels, stars, or human armies.

    H3742 כְּרוּב (keruv) - "Cherub"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration keruv
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 91 occurrences
    Definition "a cherub or imaginary figure"

    Key Usages: - Guarding Eden (Gen 3:24) - On the ark of the covenant (Exo 25:18-22) - In Ezekiel's visions (Ezek 1, 10) - "The anointed cherub" (Ezek 28:14)

    Note: A specific class of angelic beings, NEVER used for humans.

    H8314 שָׂרָף (saraph) - "Seraph"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration saraph
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 7 occurrences
    Definition "burning; (figuratively) poisonous (serpent); specifically, a seraph or symbolical creature"

    Key Usages: - Isaiah 6:2,6 - Celestial beings around God's throne

    Note: A specific class of angelic beings, NEVER used for humans.

    PART C: Greek Terms for God's Human People¶

    G5207 υἱός (huios) - "Son"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration huios
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 382 occurrences
    Definition "a 'son' (sometimes of animals), used very widely of immediate, remote or figuratively, kinship"

    Key Usages for Humans: - "Sons of God" (huioi theou) - Rom 8:14,19; Gal 3:26 - "Son of David" - Jesus' messianic title - "Sons of the kingdom" (Matt 8:12)

    G5043 τέκνον (teknon) - "Child"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration teknon
    Part of Speech Neuter noun
    BLB Count 99 occurrences
    Definition "a child (as produced)"

    Key Usages: - "Children of God" (tekna theou) - John 1:12; Rom 8:16,21; 1 John 3:1-2,10 - "Children of the resurrection" (Luke 20:36) - Emphasizes origin/relationship (vs. huios which emphasizes status/rights)

    G40 ἅγιος (hagios) - "Holy/Saint"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration hagios
    Part of Speech Adjective
    BLB Count 229 occurrences
    Definition "sacred (physically, pure, morally blameless or religious, ceremonially, consecrated)"

    Key Usages for Humans: - "Saints" (hagioi) - Standard NT term for believers (Eph 1:1; Rom 1:7) - "Holy nation" (ethnos hagion) - 1 Pet 2:9

    Note: Like Hebrew qadosh, this term applies to BOTH humans and angels: - "Holy angels" (angeloi hagioi) - Matt 25:31; Mark 8:38

    G1577 ἐκκλησία (ekklesia) - "Church/Assembly"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration ekklesia
    Part of Speech Feminine noun
    BLB Count 118 occurrences
    Definition "a calling out, i.e. a popular meeting, especially a religious congregation"

    Key Usages: - "The church" (he ekklesia) - The body of believers - "Church of God" (ekklesia theou) - Acts 20:28; 1 Cor 1:2 - "Churches of Christ" (ekklesiai Christou) - Rom 16:16

    Note: This term is EXCLUSIVELY for human assemblies/believers, NEVER for angels.

    G2992 λαός (laos) - "People"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration laos
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 143 occurrences
    Definition "a people (in general)"

    Key Usages: - "His people" - Israel or the church - "A peculiar people" (laon periousios) - Titus 2:14

    Note: Greek equivalent of Hebrew am. EXCLUSIVELY for humans.

    G5206 υἱοθεσία (huiothesia) - "Adoption"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration huiothesia
    Part of Speech Feminine noun
    BLB Count 5 occurrences
    Definition "the placing as a son, adoption"

    Key Usages: - Rom 8:15 - "Spirit of adoption" - Rom 8:23 - "Waiting for the adoption" - Rom 9:4 - Israel's adoption - Gal 4:5 - "Adoption of sons" - Eph 1:5 - Predestined to adoption

    Note: This term is EXCLUSIVELY for humans. Angels are never "adopted" - they were created as what they are.

    PART D: Greek Terms for Angels¶

    G32 ἄγγελος (aggelos) - "Angel/Messenger"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration aggelos
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 186 occurrences
    Definition "a messenger; especially an 'angel'; by implication, a pastor"

    Key Usages: - "Angel of the Lord" (aggelos kyriou) - "Angels of God" (angeloi theou) - "His angels" (angeloi autou) - "Angels of the churches" (aggeloi ton ekklesion) - Rev 2-3

    Note: Can refer to human messengers in some contexts (Luke 7:24).

    G743 ἀρχάγγελος (archangelos) - "Archangel"¶

    Aspect Value
    Transliteration archangelos
    Part of Speech Masculine noun
    BLB Count 2 occurrences
    Definition "a chief angel"

    Key Usages: - 1 Thess 4:16 - "The voice of the archangel" - Jude 1:9 - "Michael the archangel"

    Note: EXCLUSIVELY for angels. The definite article ("THE archangel") suggests singularity.
