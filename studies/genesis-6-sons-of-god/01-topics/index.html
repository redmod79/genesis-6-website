    Topics Research: Identity of "Sons of God" in Genesis 6¶

    Question¶

    Who are the "sons of God" (bene elohim) in Genesis 6:2,4?

    Purpose¶

    To examine the biblical evidence for the identity of the "sons of God" in Genesis 6, using Scripture to interpret Scripture, without relying on extra-biblical sources as authoritative.

    Prerequisite Studies¶

    This study builds on the following completed studies:

    Study Key Finding
    1-peter-3-spirits-in-prison "Spirits in prison" were humans who rejected Noah's preaching; Christ preached through Noah by His Spirit
    2-peter-2-4-angels-that-sinned Describes angelic rebellion (Rev 12); "chains of darkness" = spiritual state; Genesis 6 connection NOT established by text
    jude-6-7-angels-sin Angels abandoned position/dwelling (rebellion); "strange flesh" = homosexuality; Genesis 6 connection NOT required
    moses-angel-terminology Moses uses "malak" (angel) for celestial beings - 28+ times; does NOT use "sons of God" for angels
    moses-human-god-relationship-terms Moses uses various terms for godly humans; "sons of God" appears only in Gen 6:2,4

    The Two Major Interpretive Views¶

    View 1: Angels (Supernatural Beings)¶

    • "Sons of God" = angels who took human wives
    • Supported by: Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7 usage; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 6
    • Produces: Nephilim (giants) as hybrid offspring

    View 2: Sethite Line (Godly Humans)¶

    • "Sons of God" = descendants of Seth who intermarried with ungodly Cainites
    • Supported by: Genesis 4-5 context; NT usage of "sons/children of God" for believers
    • Produces: Nephilim as mighty men from godly-ungodly union

    Key Research Questions¶

    1. How is "sons of God" (bene elohim) used in the Old Testament?
    2. How is "sons of God" used in the New Testament?
    3. What does the immediate context of Genesis 4-6 indicate?
    4. What does Moses's vocabulary usage indicate?
    5. What does Jesus teach about angels and marriage?
    6. Do 2 Peter 2:4 and Jude 6 require a Genesis 6 connection?

    Nave's Topical Bible - Relevant Topics¶

    ANGEL¶

    • Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7 - "sons of God" presenting before the LORD
    • Do not marry (Matt 22:30; Mark 12:25)
    • Various orders and functions

    ADOPTION (Spiritual)¶

    • DEU 14:1 - "children of the LORD your God"
    • ROM 8:14-19 - "sons of God" for believers
    • GAL 3:26; 4:5-7 - adoption as sons
    • 1JN 3:1,2 - "called the sons of God"

    GIANTS¶

    • GEN 6:4 - Nephilim in earth before and after
    • NUM 13:33 - descendants of Anak
    • Various giant races: Emim, Anakim, Zamzummim, Rephaim

    Methodology¶

    1. Examine ALL biblical occurrences of "sons of God"
    2. Analyze Genesis 4-6 context (Cain and Seth lines)
    3. Apply findings from prerequisite studies
    4. Evaluate each view against the full biblical evidence
    5. Present conclusion based on Scripture alone
