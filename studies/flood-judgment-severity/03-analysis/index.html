    Verse Analysis: Severity of the Flood Judgment¶

    The Argument Examined¶

    The Angel View Claim¶

    "A worldwide flood seems extreme for godly-ungodly intermarriage. Israel later intermarried with pagans without such judgment. The flood's severity proves the corruption was genetic (angel-human hybrids), not just moral."

    The Question¶

    Does the text support this claim? Or does it give different reasons for the flood's severity?

    Part 1: What Genesis 6 Actually Says¶

    God's Stated Reasons for the Flood¶

    Verse Statement Category
    6:5 "the wickedness of man was great in the earth" Moral
    6:5 "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually" Moral
    6:11 "The earth also was corrupt before God" Moral
    6:11 "the earth was filled with violence" Moral
    6:12 "all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth" Moral
    6:13 "the earth is filled with violence through them" Moral

    What is NOT Stated¶

    The text does NOT say: - "Because angels married humans" - "Because of genetic corruption" - "Because of angel-human hybrids" - "Because the human gene pool was contaminated" - Any reference to DNA, genetics, or biological contamination

    Finding: Every stated reason is moral, not genetic.

    Part 2: The Key Hebrew Words¶

    "Corrupt" - שָׁחַת (shachath, H7843)¶

    Meaning: To destroy, corrupt, spoil, ruin (morally)

    Usage in Genesis 6: - v.11: "The earth also was corrupt [shachath]" - v.12: "all flesh had corrupted [shachath] his way"

    Other uses showing moral meaning: - Exodus 32:7 - Israel "corrupted themselves" (golden calf) - moral - Deuteronomy 4:16 - "corrupt yourselves" (making idols) - moral - Judges 2:19 - "corrupted themselves more than their fathers" - moral

    Finding: Shachath consistently refers to moral/spiritual corruption, not genetic.

    "Perfect" - תָּמִים (tamim, H8549)¶

    Meaning: Complete, whole, without blemish, having integrity

    Usage in Genesis 6:9: - "Noah was a just man and perfect [tamim] in his generations"

    Other uses showing moral meaning: - Genesis 17:1 - "walk before me, and be thou perfect" (to Abraham) - moral command - Deuteronomy 18:13 - "Thou shalt be perfect with the LORD" - moral command - Psalm 37:37 - "Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright" - moral character

    Finding: Tamim describes moral integrity, not genetic purity.

    "In His Generations" - בְּדֹרֹתָיו (bedorotav)¶

    Meaning: "Among his contemporaries" or "in his time"

    Compare with Genesis 7:1:

    "thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation"

    Finding: Noah was righteous compared to his contemporaries, not genetically distinct.

    "Violence" - חָמָס (chamas, H2555)¶

    Meaning: Violence, wrong, cruelty, unjust gain

    Usage in Genesis 6: - v.11: "the earth was filled with violence [chamas]" - v.13: "the earth is filled with violence [chamas]"

    This is clearly moral/behavioral - referring to violent actions, not genetic states.

    "Wickedness" - רָעָה (ra'ah, H7451)¶

    Meaning: Bad, evil, wickedness (moral evil)

    Usage in Genesis 6:5: - "the wickedness [ra'ah] of man was great"

    This is quintessential moral vocabulary.

    Part 3: The Unique Severity of Pre-Flood Sin¶

    Why Was the Flood More Severe Than Other Judgments?¶

    The key is universality. Note the language:

    Phrase Significance
    "Every imagination" Not some, but all
    "Only evil" No good mixed in
    "Continually" Without pause or cessation
    "All flesh" Universal scope
    "The earth was filled" Global extent

    This describes TOTAL moral collapse - not a localized sin problem.

    Comparison with Other Intermarriages¶

    Situation Sin Extent Faithful Remaining Judgment
    Genesis 6 Intermarriage + total corruption Universal - "all flesh" Only Noah Worldwide flood
    Numbers 25 Intermarriage + idolatry Localized Many 24,000 died
    Judges 3 Intermarriage + idolatry National Many 8 years bondage
    1 Kings 11 Intermarriage + idolatry Individual Many Kingdom divided
    Ezra 10 Intermarriage Community Many Divorce required

    Finding: The difference is not the TYPE of sin but the EXTENT of corruption.

    Part 4: Jesus's Description of Noah's Days¶

    What Jesus Emphasizes (Matthew 24:37-39; Luke 17:26-27)¶

    • Eating and drinking - normal life activities
    • Marrying and giving in marriage - normal marriage
    • Knew not - spiritual ignorance and unreadiness

    What Jesus Does NOT Mention¶

    • Angel-human hybridization
    • Genetic corruption
    • Nephilim or giants
    • Supernatural unions
    • DNA contamination

    Finding: Jesus describes normal human activities done in comfortable sin and spiritual ignorance. This perfectly fits the Sethite view: the godly line intermarried with the ungodly, spiritual discernment was lost, people lived in comfortable sin unaware of coming judgment.

    Part 5: The New Testament Explanation¶

    2 Peter 2:5¶

    "And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly"

    Key term: "ungodly" (ἀσεβής, asebeis) - irreverent, wicked

    This is moral language, not genetic.

    Hebrews 11:7¶

    "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world..."

    Noah's faith condemned "the world" - moral contrast, not genetic.

    Part 6: How Intermarriage Led to Universal Corruption¶

    The Mechanism¶

    1. The godly line maintained worship of the LORD (Gen 4:26 - "then began men to call upon the name of the LORD")
    2. Intermarriage with the ungodly line diluted spiritual commitment
    3. Each generation became more corrupt (cf. Judges 2:19 pattern)
    4. Eventually NO ONE except Noah remained faithful
    5. Universal wickedness resulted - "every imagination only evil continually"

    The Pattern Repeats in Scripture¶

    Solomon: Married foreign wives → heart turned to idols → kingdom divided

    Israel in Canaan: Intermarried → served foreign gods → cycles of judgment

    Post-exilic community: Intermarried → Ezra/Nehemiah's reforms

    The pre-flood situation was the first and most complete expression of this pattern. Starting from only one godly line (Seth's), intermarriage with the ungodly eventually eliminated ALL faithful people except one family.

    Part 7: Why the Flood Was Necessary¶

    The Angel View Claims¶

    The flood was necessary because: - Human DNA was contaminated by angel genetics - Only Noah's family had pure human genetics - The gene pool had to be cleansed

    Problem: The text never mentions genetics. This is modern speculation imposed on ancient text.

    What the Text Actually Shows¶

    The flood was necessary because: - Every imagination of man's heart was only evil continually (v.5) - All flesh had corrupted his way (v.12) - The earth was filled with violence (v.11, 13) - Only Noah was righteous (v.8-9)

    There was no one left to preserve righteousness except Noah. The infection was moral, and it had become universal. A fresh start was needed.

    Part 8: Connection to Related Studies¶

    genesis-6-sons-of-god Study¶

    That study identified an unresolved question for the Sethite view:

    "Why would mixed marriage warrant such severe judgment?"

    This study answers: It wasn't "just" intermarriage - it was the RESULT: universal moral corruption. Other intermarriages brought lesser judgments because the corruption was localized. Pre-flood corruption was TOTAL - "every imagination only evil continually."

    nephilim-origin Study¶

    That study established: - Nephilim existed BEFORE and AFTER the "sons of God" events - Nephilim were large humans (pre-flood longevity), not angel hybrids - The timing problem: "There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that"

    This confirms the flood wasn't about destroying angel-human hybrids but judging universal human wickedness.

    Summary of Analysis¶

    The Claim vs. The Evidence¶

    Claim Evidence
    "Genetic corruption required the flood" Text gives moral reasons only
    "'Corrupt' means genetic contamination" Shachath = moral corruption throughout Scripture
    "'Perfect' means genetically pure" Tamim = moral integrity in all other uses
    "Jesus warned of angel-human unions" Jesus described normal human activities
    "NT supports genetic view" NT describes "the world of the ungodly"

    Why the Flood Was Severe¶

    Factor Pre-Flood Other Intermarriages
    Extent Universal - "all flesh" Localized
    Depth "Every imagination only evil" Partial corruption
    Remaining faithful Only Noah Many righteous remained
    Violence Earth "filled with violence" Localized violence
    Remedy possible? No - too far gone Yes - reform possible

    The flood's severity matches the totality of moral corruption, not genetic contamination.
