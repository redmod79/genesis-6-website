    Conclusion: Severity of the Flood Judgment¶

    Question¶

    Why was the flood so severe? Does the worldwide destruction require genetic corruption (angel-human hybrids) to explain it, or does the text give different reasons?

    Summary Answer¶

    The claim that "only genetic corruption explains the flood's severity" fails because the text explicitly gives moral reasons for the flood: wickedness, evil thoughts, corruption, and violence. The Hebrew words used (shachath, tamim, chamas, ra'ah) consistently refer to moral qualities, not genetics. The flood's severity matches the universality of the moral corruption - "every imagination only evil continually" and "all flesh had corrupted his way." Other intermarriages in Scripture received lesser judgments because the corruption was localized; the pre-flood corruption was total.

    Key Verses¶

    Genesis 6:5 And GOD saw that the wickedness of man [was] great in the earth, and [that] every imagination of the thoughts of his heart [was] only evil continually.

    Genesis 6:11 The earth also was corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence.

    Genesis 6:12 And God looked upon the earth, and, behold, it was corrupt; for all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth.

    2 Peter 2:5 And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth [person], a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly.

    God's Stated Reasons for the Flood¶

    Verse Reason Category
    Genesis 6:5 "Wickedness of man was great" Moral
    Genesis 6:5 "Every imagination... only evil continually" Moral
    Genesis 6:11 "Earth was corrupt before God" Moral
    Genesis 6:11 "Earth was filled with violence" Moral
    Genesis 6:12 "All flesh had corrupted his way" Moral
    Genesis 6:13 "Earth is filled with violence" Moral

    NOT stated: genetics, DNA, angel-human hybrids, biological contamination.

    Why Other Intermarriages Had Lesser Judgments¶

    Situation Extent Judgment
    Genesis 6 Universal - "all flesh," "every imagination" Worldwide flood
    Numbers 25 (Baal Peor) Localized 24,000 died
    Judges 3 (Canaanites) National 8 years bondage
    1 Kings 11 (Solomon) Individual Kingdom divided
    Ezra 10 Community Divorce required

    The difference is not the TYPE of sin but the EXTENT of corruption.

    The pre-flood situation was unique: - Every imagination only evil - All flesh corrupted - Only Noah righteous - Earth filled with violence

    No other intermarriage situation produced TOTAL moral collapse.

    Word Studies Summary¶

    Term Hebrew Meaning Category
    Wickedness רָעָה (ra'ah) evil, badness Moral
    Corrupt שָׁחַת (shachath) ruin, spoil morally Moral
    Violence חָמָס (chamas) violence, wrong Moral
    Perfect תָּמִים (tamim) complete, integrity Moral

    "Corrupt" (shachath) = Moral, Not Genetic¶

    The Hebrew shachath is used throughout Scripture for moral corruption: - Exodus 32:7 - Israel "corrupted themselves" with the golden calf - Deuteronomy 4:16 - "corrupt yourselves" by making idols - Judges 2:19 - "corrupted themselves more than their fathers"

    Never used for genetic or biological corruption.

    "Perfect in His Generations" (tamim) = Moral Integrity¶

    Genesis 6:9: Noah was "perfect (tamim) in his generations"

    Tamim means moral integrity, not genetic purity: - Genesis 17:1 - Abraham told to "be perfect" (moral command) - Deuteronomy 18:13 - "be perfect with the LORD" (moral) - Psalm 37:37 - "Mark the perfect man" (righteous character)

    Noah was morally upright among his corrupt contemporaries.

    Jesus's Description of Noah's Days¶

    Matthew 24:37-38 - "For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark"

    Jesus emphasizes: - Normal life activities (eating, drinking) - Normal marriage (marrying, giving in marriage) - Spiritual ignorance ("knew not until the flood came")

    Jesus does NOT mention: - Angel-human hybridization - Genetic corruption - Nephilim or giants

    This fits the Sethite view: the godly line intermarried with the ungodly, spiritual discernment was lost, people lived in comfortable sin unaware of judgment.

    How Intermarriage Led to Universal Corruption¶

    The mechanism:

    1. Godly line (Seth) maintained worship of the LORD (Gen 4:26)
    2. Intermarriage with ungodly line (Cain) diluted commitment
    3. Each generation grew more corrupt
    4. Eventually NO ONE except Noah remained faithful
    5. Result: "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually"

    This pattern repeats in Scripture: - Solomon's wives turned his heart (1 Kings 11) - Israel intermarried and served foreign gods (Judges 3) - Post-exilic community intermarried (Ezra 10)

    The pre-flood was the first and most complete expression - the ENTIRE world corrupted.

    The Real Explanation for Severity¶

    The flood was severe because:

    1. The corruption was universal - not localized like later intermarriages
    2. No faithful remained - only Noah's family
    3. Every thought was evil - total depravity
    4. Violence filled the earth - society completely broken
    5. No remedy was possible - too far gone for reform

    The severity matches the totality of moral corruption, not genetic contamination.

    Answer: It wasn't the intermarriage itself but the RESULT - universal moral corruption. Other intermarriages brought lesser judgments because the corruption was localized. Pre-flood corruption was TOTAL.

    nephilim-origin Study¶

    This confirms the flood wasn't about destroying angel-human hybrids but judging universal human wickedness.

    Final Assessment¶

    The angel view's claim that "only genetic corruption explains the flood's severity" fails because:

    1. The text gives clear reasons - wickedness, evil, corruption, violence (all moral terms)
    2. The Hebrew words (shachath, tamim) denote moral qualities, not genetics
    3. The severity matches the universality - "every imagination," "all flesh"
    4. Jesus describes normal human activities, not supernatural corruption
    5. Other intermarriages had lesser judgments because corruption was localized

    The Sethite view adequately explains the flood's severity: the godly line intermarried with the ungodly, moral corruption spread until it became universal, and only Noah remained righteous. This required a complete restart.

    No "genetic corruption" theory is needed - the text explains itself.
