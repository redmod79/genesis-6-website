    Topics Research: Origin of the Nephilim¶

    Question¶

    The angel view claims the Nephilim were angel-human hybrids, explaining their extraordinary size. If the "sons of God" were just humans (Sethite view), how do we explain the giants?

    Semantic Search Results¶

    Nave's Topical Dictionary does not contain an entry for "Nephilim" directly. The following related topics were found:

    Topic Relevance
    GIANTS Primary topic - lists all giant-related entries
    ANAKIM Race of giants defeated by Joshua and Caleb
    EMIMS Race of giants in Moab
    ZAMZUMMIMS Race of giants in Ammon
    REPHAIM Ancient giant people of Canaan
    OG Giant king of Bashan
    GOLIATH Giant champion of Gath
    ANAK Father of three giants

    Selected Topics¶

    GIANTS¶

    General scriptures concerning GEN 6:4 See ANAKIM See EMIMS See GOLIATH See ISHBI-BENOB See OG See REPHAIM See ZAMZUMMIM

    Key observation: Nave's treats Genesis 6:4 as the foundational reference for giants, then points to specific post-flood giant peoples.

    ANAKIM¶

    A race of giants NUM 13:28-33; DEU 1:28; 2:10; 9:2 Defeated by Joshua JOS 11:21,22 Defeated by Caleb JOS 14:12,15; 15:13,14; JDG 1:20 See HEBRON

    Key observation: The Anakim were a "race" - a human ethnic group. Their lineage is traced (Arba > Anak > sons). No angel connection mentioned.

    ANAK¶

    Father of three giants JOS 15:13,14; 21:11

    Key observation: Anak was a human father whose children were giants. Human genealogy, not angelic.

    EMIMS¶

    A race of giants GEN 14:5; DEU 2:10,11

    Key observation: Called "a race" - human tribe. Compared to the Anakim in stature but given their own tribal name by the Moabites.

    ZAMZUMMIMS¶

    Also called ZUZIMS A race of giants GEN 14:5; DEU 2:20,21

    Key observation: Another "race" of giants. Human tribe destroyed by the LORD.

    REPHAIM¶

    1. An ancient people of Palestine, of great stature GEN 14:5; 15:20; DEU 2:11,20; 3:11,13; JOS 12:4; 13:12; 17:15
    2. A valley; the boundary between the territories of Judah and Benjamin JOS 15:8; 18:16 The battleground of David and the Philistines 2SA 5:18,22; 23:13; 1CH 11:15; 14:9 The productiveness of ISA 17:5

    Key observation: Listed among Canaanite tribes (Gen 15:20) alongside Hittites, Perizzites, etc. - all human peoples.

    OG¶

    (King of Bashan) A man of gigantic stature NUM 21:33; DEU 3:11; JOS 12:4; 13:12 Defeated and killed by Moses NUM 21:33-35; DEU 1:4; 3:1-7; 29:7; 31:4; JOS 2:10; 9:10; PSA 135:10,11; 136:18-20 Land of, given to the tribes of Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh...

    Key observation: Og was "a man" - explicitly human. "Remnant of giants" indicates he descended from earlier large humans.

    GOLIATH¶

    (A giant champion of Gath) Defied armies of Israel and is killed by David 1SA 17; 21:9; 22:10 His sons 2SA 21:15-22; 1CH 20:4-8

    Key observation: Philistine warrior. Human nationality. His "sons" - human family lineage.
