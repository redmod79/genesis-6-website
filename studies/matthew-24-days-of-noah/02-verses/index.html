    Key Observations from the Texts¶

    What Jesus DID Say:¶

    1. People were living ordinary lives - eating, drinking, marrying
    2. They "knew not" - were unaware/unconcerned
    3. Judgment came suddenly
    4. Noah entered the ark as the dividing point
    5. Destruction was complete ("took them all away," "destroyed them all")

    What Jesus DID NOT Say:¶

    1. No mention of "sons of God"
    2. No mention of "daughters of men"
    3. No mention of Nephilim or giants
    4. No mention of angels
    5. No mention of supernatural mixing
    6. No mention of corruption of bloodlines
    7. No mention of unusual wickedness beyond ordinary life

    The Lot Parallel (Luke 17:28-29)¶

    Jesus parallels Noah's days with Lot's days. In Lot's case: - They were "eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building" - Destruction came the day Lot left - Fire destroyed them all

    Critical point: The Lot parallel proves Jesus's point was about ordinary life interrupted by sudden judgment, NOT about any supernatural element unique to Noah's time.
