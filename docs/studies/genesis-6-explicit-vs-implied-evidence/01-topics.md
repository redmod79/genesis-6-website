# Nave's Topical Index: Explicit vs. Implied Evidence for Genesis 6 "Sons of God"

## Relevant Topics

This is a methodological study rather than a topical one. The relevant Nave's categories are:

### ANGELS
- Nature as spirits: Hebrews 1:7, 14
- Physical appearances: Genesis 18-19
- Do not marry: Matthew 22:30; Mark 12:25; Luke 20:35-36
- Rebellion: 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6; Revelation 12:4, 7-9
- "Sons of God" in Job: Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7

### SONS OF GOD
- In Job (heavenly beings): Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7
- In Genesis 6 (disputed): Genesis 6:2, 4
- In Deuteronomy (humans): Deuteronomy 14:1
- In NT (human believers): John 1:12; Romans 8:14, 19; Galatians 3:26; 1 John 3:1-2; Philippians 2:15

### MARRIAGE
- Institution: Genesis 2:24
- Normal vocabulary: "took wives" (laqach nashim)
- Angels and marriage: Matthew 22:30; Mark 12:25; Luke 20:35-36
- Intermarriage prohibition: Deuteronomy 7:3-4; Exodus 34:15-16

### GIANTS / NEPHILIM
- Pre-flood: Genesis 6:4
- Post-flood (Anakim): Numbers 13:33; Joshua 15:13-14
- Human genealogies: Deuteronomy 2:10-11, 20-21; 2 Samuel 21:16-22

### FLOOD
- Moral reasons: Genesis 6:5, 11-13
- "Man" and "flesh": Genesis 6:3
- Violence: Genesis 6:11, 13

### CORRUPTION
- Moral corruption: Genesis 6:5, 11-12; Deuteronomy 9:12; 31:29; Judges 2:19
- "Corrupt his way" = always moral in Scripture

### HERMENEUTICS / METHODOLOGY
- Explicit vs. implied evidence
- Clear texts interpret unclear texts
- Jesus's authority over OT interpretation
- Context determines meaning

---

## Note

This study is methodological in nature. The topics listed above represent the biblical categories that contain the arguments being evaluated. The study does not add new topical research but instead categorizes the evidence from 26 prerequisite studies as either EXPLICIT (directly stated) or IMPLIED (requiring inference).
