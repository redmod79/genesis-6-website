# Word Studies: Identity of the "Sons of God" in Genesis 6

## Primary Terms in Genesis 6:1-4

### בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים (bene ha'elohim) - "Sons of God"

**Strong's H1121 + H430**

**Components:**
- בֵּן (ben, H1121) - son, child, descendant
- אֱלֹהִים (elohim, H430) - God, gods, divine beings

**All Five OT Occurrences:**

| Passage | Context | Likely Referent |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Genesis 6:2 | Earthly marriage scene | Disputed |
| Genesis 6:4 | Earthly marriage scene | Disputed |
| Job 1:6 | Heavenly throne room with Satan | Heavenly beings |
| Job 2:1 | Heavenly throne room with Satan | Heavenly beings |
| Job 38:7 | Creation, before humans existed | Heavenly beings |

**Key Observation:** Job's context is heavenly throne-room scenes where Satan appears. Genesis 6's context is earthly marriage. Different contexts may indicate different referents.

---

### בָּשָׂר (basar) - "Flesh"

**Strong's H1320**

**Definition:** Flesh, body; used for human physical nature; living beings generally

**Genesis 6:3:** "for that **he also is flesh** (בָּשָׂר)"

**Key Usage:**
- "He also" (הוּא גַּם) = the "sons of God" are ALSO flesh
- This identifies them with humanity, not angels
- Angels are never called "flesh" in Scripture

**Related Usages:**

| Passage | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Genesis 2:21 | Adam's flesh - human body |
| Genesis 2:24 | "One flesh" - marriage union |
| Genesis 6:12 | "All flesh had corrupted his way" |
| Genesis 6:13 | "The end of all flesh is come" |
| Isaiah 40:6 | "All flesh is grass" - human mortality |

**Key Finding:** "Flesh" consistently describes human physical nature. God calling the "sons of God" flesh in Genesis 6:3 identifies them as human.

---

### מַלְאָךְ (mal'ak) - "Angel/Messenger"

**Strong's H4397**

**Definition:** Messenger, angel; envoy (human or divine)

**Moses's Usage in Pentateuch:**
- "Angel of the LORD" (מַלְאָךְ יְהוָה) - 14 times
- "Angel of God" (מַלְאָךְ אֱלֹהִים) - 3 times
- "Angels" (מַלְאָכִים) - 4 times
- Other angel references - 7 times

**Key Observation:** When Moses wants to indicate a celestial being, he uses "malak" - consistently. He never uses "bene elohim" for angels anywhere in the Pentateuch.

**The Question:** If Moses meant angels in Genesis 6:2, why didn't he write "the angels of God" (מַלְאָכֵי הָאֱלֹהִים)?

---

### נְפִלִים (nephilim) - "Giants"

**Strong's H5303**

**Definition:** From H5307 (naphal = to fall); translated "giants" in KJV

**Etymology Possibilities:**
- "Fallen ones" (from naphal)
- "Those who cause to fall" (tyrants, bullies)
- Simply a descriptor for their imposing size

**Usage:**

| Passage | Context |
|---------|---------|
| Genesis 6:4 | Pre-flood giants (and post-flood: "also after that") |
| Numbers 13:33 | The Anakim called "nephilim" |

**Key Finding:** The Anakim in Numbers 13:33 are called "nephilim" but have fully traceable human genealogy (Arba → Anak → sons). This proves "nephilim" describes what they WERE (giants), not HOW they originated (not requiring angel parentage).

---

### שָׁחַת (shachath) - "Corrupt"

**Strong's H7843**

**Definition:** To ruin, destroy, corrupt; when describing self-corruption, always MORAL

**Genesis 6:11-12:** "corrupt (נִשְׁחָתָה)... all flesh had corrupted (הִשְׁחִית)"

**All Parallel Usages (People Corrupting Themselves):**

| Passage | Context | Type |
|---------|---------|------|
| Exodus 32:7 | Golden calf | **Moral** |
| Deuteronomy 4:16 | Making idols | **Moral** |
| Deuteronomy 4:25 | Provoking God | **Moral** |
| Deuteronomy 9:12 | Golden calf | **Moral** |
| Deuteronomy 31:29 | Future apostasy | **Moral** |
| Deuteronomy 32:5 | "Perverse generation" | **Moral** |
| Judges 2:19 | Following false gods | **Moral** |
| Hosea 9:9 | "Days of Gibeah" | **Moral** |

**Key Finding:** When this word describes people corrupting **themselves**, it ALWAYS refers to **MORAL** corruption - never genetic/biological.

---

### דֶּרֶךְ (derek) - "Way"

**Strong's H1870**

**Definition:** Way, road, manner, journey; figuratively: manner of life, conduct

**Genesis 6:12:** "all flesh had corrupted his **way** (דַּרְכּוֹ)"

**Usage Pattern:**

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| "Way of the LORD" | God's standards | Genesis 18:19 |
| "Corrupt his way" | Ruined conduct | Genesis 6:12 |
| "Way of sinners" | Sinful lifestyle | Psalm 1:1 |
| "Way of life" | Manner of living | Proverbs 6:23 |

**Key Finding:** "Way" (derek) describes manner of life, conduct, behavior - never genetics or biology.

---

### תָּמִים (tamim) - "Perfect"

**Strong's H8549**

**Definition:** Complete, whole, entire; having integrity; without blemish (moral or physical depending on context)

**Genesis 6:9:** "Noah was... **perfect** (תָּמִים) in his generations"

**Usage for MORAL Integrity:**

| Passage | Context | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Genesis 17:1 | Abraham told to "be perfect" | **Moral** command |
| Deuteronomy 18:13 | "Be perfect with the LORD" | **Moral** conduct |
| 2 Samuel 22:24 | "I was also upright" | **Moral** character |
| Psalm 37:37 | "Mark the perfect man" | **Righteous** character |
| Psalm 101:2 | "I will walk... with a perfect heart" | **Moral** integrity |
| Proverbs 11:5 | "The righteousness of the perfect" | **Moral** quality |

**Key Finding:** When "tamim" describes a person's character, it means moral integrity. Noah was morally upright among his corrupt contemporaries - not genetically pure.

---

### חָמָס (chamas) - "Violence"

**Strong's H2555**

**Definition:** Violence, wrong, cruelty; injustice; oppression

**Genesis 6:11, 13:** "filled with **violence** (חָמָס)"

**Significance:** Violence is BEHAVIOR, not genetics. God twice emphasizes violence as the reason for judgment - describing what people DID, not what they were biologically.

## Key Greek Terms

### ἄγγελος (angelos) - "Angel"

**Strong's G32**

**Definition:** Messenger, angel (human or divine)

**LXX Usage:**

| Passage | Hebrew | Greek LXX | Translation |
|---------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Genesis 6:2 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ | "sons of God" |
| Job 1:6 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ | "angels of God" |
| Job 2:1 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ | "angels of God" |
| Job 38:7 | בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים | ἀγγέλους μου | "my angels" |

**Key Finding:** The LXX translators chose DIFFERENT Greek words for the same Hebrew phrase based on context. They translated Genesis 6:2 as "sons" but Job as "angels" - showing they saw a contextual difference.

---

### πνεῦμα (pneuma) - "Spirit"

**Strong's G4151**

**Definition:** Spirit, wind, breath; used for the Holy Spirit, human spirits, and angelic beings

**Hebrews 1:7:** "Who maketh his angels **spirits** (πνεύματα)"

**Hebrews 1:14:** "Are they not all ministering **spirits** (πνεύματα)"

**Key Finding:** Angels are defined as "spirits" by nature. They can appear in physical form (Genesis 18-19) but their essential nature is spirit, not flesh.

---

### σὰρξ ἑτέρα (sarx hetera) - "Strange Flesh"

**Strong's G4561 + G2087**

**Jude 7:** "going after **strange flesh** (σαρκὸς ἑτέρας)"

**Analysis:**
- σάρξ (sarx) = flesh
- ἕτερος (heteros) = other, different (qualitatively)

**What Does "Different Flesh" Mean?**

The men of Sodom pursued **men**, not the natural female counterpart (Genesis 19:4-5). From God's design perspective:
- Man + Woman = natural union
- Man + Man = "different/other" flesh

Romans 1:26-27 describes this as "against nature."

**Key Finding:** "Strange flesh" describes homosexuality, not pursuit of angelic beings. The Sodomites didn't know the visitors were angels.

---

### ἀρχή (arche) - "First Estate"

**Strong's G746**

**Definition:** Beginning, origin; first place; principality, rule, domain

**Jude 6:** "the angels which kept not their **first estate** (ἀρχὴν)"

**What Did the Angels Abandon?**
- Their ἀρχή = their position, rank, domain
- This describes abandoning their role, not changing their nature
- Revelation 12 clarifies: they were "cast out" of heaven

**Key Finding:** The angels abandoned their position in the war against God (Revelation 12), not their physical nature.

---

### οἰκητήριον (oiketerion) - "Habitation"

**Strong's G3613**

**Definition:** Dwelling place, habitation

**Jude 6:** "left their own **habitation** (οἰκητήριον)"

**Only Other NT Occurrence:** 2 Corinthians 5:2 - the resurrection body as our heavenly "house"

**What Did They Leave?**
- Their dwelling place = heaven
- Revelation 12:8: "neither was their place found any more in heaven"

**Key Finding:** The angels left heaven (were cast out), not that they left their spiritual nature.

---

## Word Studies Summary

### Terms That Identify the "Sons of God" as Human

| Term | Evidence |
|------|----------|
| בָּשָׂר (basar) | God calls them "flesh" in 6:3 |
| אָדָם (adam) | God addresses "man" in 6:3 |
| תָּמִים (tamim) | Noah's "perfection" is moral, not genetic |
| שָׁחַת (shachath) | "Corrupt" in parallels is always moral |
| דֶּרֶךְ (derek) | "Way" means conduct, not genetics |
| חָמָס (chamas) | "Violence" is behavior, not biology |

### Terms That Show Angels Don't Marry

| Term | Evidence |
|------|----------|
| πνεῦμα (pneuma) | Angels are "spirits" by nature |
| ἰσάγγελοι (isangeloi) | Resurrected saints "equal to angels" - won't marry |
| ἄγγελοι... ἐν οὐρανῷ | "Angels in heaven" don't marry (Matt 22:30) |

### Terms That Don't Prove the Angel View

| Term | Claimed Meaning | Actual Meaning |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| σὰρξ ἑτέρα | Angelic flesh | Homosexuality (men pursuing men) |
| ἀρχή | First physical state | Position, rank, domain |
| οἰκητήριον | Physical body | Dwelling place (heaven) |
| נְפִלִים | Angel-human hybrids | Giants (applied to humans with human genealogy) |
