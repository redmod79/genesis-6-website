# Verses: Deuteronomy 32:8 - "Sons of God" Textual Variant

## Primary Text - Deuteronomy 32:7-9 (KJV)

> **Deuteronomy 32:7** Remember the days of old, consider the years of many generations: ask thy father, and he will shew thee; thy elders, and they will tell thee.

> **Deuteronomy 32:8** When the **most High** divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the **children of Israel**.

> **Deuteronomy 32:9** For the **LORD'S portion** [is] his people; **Jacob** [is] the lot of his inheritance.

---

## The Textual Variant

### Masoretic Text (MT) - Hebrew

> לְמִסְפַּר בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל (lemispar bene Yisrael)
> "according to the number of the **children of Israel**"

### Dead Sea Scrolls (4QDeutj) - Hebrew

> לְמִסְפַּר בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים (lemispar bene elohim)
> "according to the number of the **sons of God**"

### Septuagint (LXX) - Greek

> κατὰ ἀριθμὸν ἀγγέλων θεοῦ (kata arithmon angelōn theou)
> "according to the number of the **angels of God**"

---

## Broader Context - Deuteronomy 32:1-12

> **Deuteronomy 32:1** Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.

> **Deuteronomy 32:4** [He is] the Rock, his work [is] perfect: for all his ways [are] judgment: a God of truth and without iniquity, just and right [is] he.

> **Deuteronomy 32:5** They have **corrupted themselves**, their spot [is] not [the spot] of his children: [they are] a perverse and crooked generation.

> **Deuteronomy 32:6** Do ye thus requite the LORD, O foolish people and unwise? [is] not he thy father [that] hath bought thee? hath he not made thee, and established thee?

> **Deuteronomy 32:10** He found him in a desert land, and in the waste howling wilderness; he led him about, he instructed him, he kept him as the apple of his eye.

> **Deuteronomy 32:12** [So] the LORD **alone** did lead him, and [there was] **no strange god with him**.

**Observation:** The song emphasizes that the LORD alone led Israel - no other gods involved.

---

## The 70 Nations - Genesis 10:32

> **Genesis 10:32** These [are] the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, in their nations: and by these were **the nations divided in the earth** after the flood.

**Observation:** Genesis 10 describes how nations were divided after the flood. Traditional counting identifies 70 nations.

---

## The 70 Israelites - Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5

> **Genesis 46:27** And the sons of Joseph, which were born him in Egypt, [were] two souls: all the souls of the house of Jacob, which came into Egypt, [were] **threescore and ten** [70].

> **Exodus 1:5** And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were **seventy souls**: for Joseph was in Egypt [already].

**Observation:** Jacob's family numbered 70 when they went to Egypt. The MT reading connects 70 nations to 70 Israelites.

---

## Parallel Passage - Deuteronomy 4:19-20

> **Deuteronomy 4:19** And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven, and when thou seest the sun, and the moon, and the stars, [even] all the host of heaven, shouldest be driven to worship them, and serve them, which **the LORD thy God hath divided unto all nations** under the whole heaven.

> **Deuteronomy 4:20** But the LORD hath taken you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, [even] out of Egypt, **to be unto him a people of inheritance**, as [ye are] this day.

**Key observations:**
- God "divided" the host of heaven to all nations (they worship them)
- Israel is God's special inheritance
- Same theme as Deuteronomy 32:8-9
- **God did the dividing** - not angels assigning themselves

## New Testament - Acts 17:26-27

> **Acts 17:26** And hath made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth, and hath **determined the times before appointed, and the bounds of their habitation**;

> **Acts 17:27** That they should seek the Lord, if haply they might feel after him, and find him, though he be not far from every one of us:

**Key observation:** Paul attributes national boundaries to **GOD**, not to angels or divine beings.

---

## "Sons of God" in Job (Heavenly Beings)

> **Job 1:6** Now there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan came also among them.

> **Job 2:1** Again there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan came also among them to present himself before the LORD.

> **Job 38:7** When the morning stars sang together, and all the **sons of God** shouted for joy?

**Observation:** These are heavenly beings in God's court - but they don't marry or reproduce. They simply attend before God.

---

## "Sons of God" Applied to Humans

### Believers Called "Sons of God"

> **John 1:12** But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the **sons of God**, [even] to them that believe on his name

> **Romans 8:14** For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the **sons of God**.

> **Galatians 3:26** For ye are all the **children of God** by faith in Christ Jesus.

### Israel Called God's Son

> **Exodus 4:22** And thou shalt say unto Pharaoh, Thus saith the LORD, **Israel [is] my son**, [even] my firstborn

> **Hosea 11:1** When Israel [was] a child, then I loved him, and called **my son** out of Egypt.

**Observation:** Both angels AND humans can be called "sons of God" - the term itself doesn't prove angelic identity.

---

## Summary of Verse Evidence

| Category | Passages | Finding |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Textual variant | Deut 32:8 | MT = "children of Israel"; DSS/LXX = "sons of God/angels" |
| 70 connection | Gen 10; 46:27; Exod 1:5 | 70 nations, 70 Israelites |
| God divides nations | Deut 4:19-20; Acts 17:26 | GOD determines national boundaries |
| Context | Deut 32:9, 12 | Israel is LORD's portion; LORD alone led |
| "Sons of God" usage | Job 1-2; John 1:12; Rom 8:14 | Used for both angels and humans |

**Even if the DSS reading is original, the verse describes God dividing nations - not angels mating with humans.**
