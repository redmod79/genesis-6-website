# Word Studies: Deuteronomy 32:8 - "Sons of God" Textual Variant

## Hebrew Terms

### עֶלְיוֹן (elyon) - "Most High"

**Strong's H5945**

- **Transliteration:** elyon
- **Pronunciation:** el-yone'
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective/Noun
- **Etymology:** From H5927 (alah - to ascend)

**Definition:** An elevation; as adjective: lofty; as divine title: the Supreme, the Most High.

**KJV Translation Frequency:**
- "high" - 18 occurrences
- "most high" - 9 occurrences
- "upper" - 8 occurrences
- Total: 53 times

**Deuteronomy 32:8 Usage:**

> "When the **most High** [עֶלְיוֹן, elyon] divided to the nations their inheritance..."

**Significance:** The subject of the verb is GOD (the Most High). Whatever the object (children of Israel or sons of God), it is GOD who performs the action of dividing nations.

---

### בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל (bene Yisrael) - "Children of Israel" (MT Reading)

**Strong's H1121 + H3478**

- **בֵּן (ben)** - son, child, descendant (H1121)
- **יִשְׂרָאֵל (Yisrael)** - Israel (H3478)

**Definition:** The descendants of Jacob/Israel; the Israelite nation.

**Usage in Deuteronomy 32:8 (MT):**

> "he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the **children of Israel** [בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל]"

**Significance:** This reading connects the 70 nations to the 70 Israelites who went to Egypt (Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5).

---

### בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים (bene elohim) - "Sons of God" (DSS Reading)

**Strong's H1121 + H430**

- **בֵּן (ben)** - son, child (H1121)
- **אֱלֹהִים (elohim)** - God, gods, divine beings (H430)

**Definition:** Literally "sons of gods/God." Used for heavenly beings in Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7.

**Dead Sea Scrolls (4QDeutj):**

> "he set the bounds of the peoples according to the number of the **sons of God** [בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים]"

**Usage Elsewhere:**

| Passage | Context | Referent |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Job 1:6 | Heavenly court | Angels presenting before God |
| Job 2:1 | Heavenly court | Angels presenting before God |
| Job 38:7 | Creation | Heavenly beings rejoicing |
| Genesis 6:2,4 | Marriages | Disputed (angels or godly line) |

**Significance:** Even in Job, the "sons of God" don't marry or reproduce - they serve in God's court.

---

### ἀγγέλων θεοῦ (angelōn theou) - "Angels of God" (LXX Reading)

**Strong's G32 + G2316**

- **ἄγγελος (angelos)** - messenger, angel (G32)
- **θεός (theos)** - God (G2316)

**Septuagint Deuteronomy 32:8:**

> "he set the boundaries of the nations according to the number of the **angels of God** [ἀγγέλων θεοῦ]"

**Significance:** The LXX translators interpreted "sons of God" as "angels" - showing this was one ancient understanding. But even angels assigned to nations don't marry humans.

---

### נָחַל (nachal) - "Divided/Inherited"

**Strong's H5157**

- **Transliteration:** nachal
- **Pronunciation:** naw-khal'
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Definition:** To inherit, get as possession; causatively: to give as inheritance

**Deuteronomy 32:8 Usage:**

> "When the most High **divided** [הַנְחֵל, hanchel] to the nations their inheritance..."

**Significance:** The action is GIVING inheritance - God distributes territories. This is administrative, not reproductive.

---

### פָּרַד (parad) - "Separated"

**Strong's H6504**

- **Transliteration:** parad
- **Pronunciation:** paw-rad'
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Definition:** To separate, divide, scatter

**Deuteronomy 32:8 Usage:**

> "when he **separated** [הַפְרִידוֹ, haphrydo] the sons of Adam..."

**Significance:** This refers to the division of humanity at Babel (Gen 10-11). God separated the nations geographically.

---

### גְּבֻל (gebul) - "Bounds/Boundaries"

**Strong's H1367**

- **Transliteration:** gebul
- **Pronunciation:** gheb-ool'
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** Border, boundary, territory

**Deuteronomy 32:8 Usage:**

> "he set the **bounds** [גְּבֻלֹת, gebulot] of the people..."

**Significance:** This is about territorial boundaries - geographic division of the earth. Not about reproduction or marriage.

**New Testament Parallel - Acts 17:26:**

> "hath determined...the **bounds** [ὁροθεσίας, horothesias] of their habitation"

Paul uses the Greek equivalent when describing God determining national boundaries.

---

### חֵלֶק (cheleq) - "Portion"

**Strong's H2506**

- **Transliteration:** cheleq
- **Pronunciation:** khay'-lek
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** Portion, share, part, territory

**Deuteronomy 32:9 Usage:**

> "For the LORD'S **portion** [חֵלֶק, cheleq] [is] his people; Jacob [is] the lot of his inheritance."

**Significance:** While other nations may have assignments (whether to Israel's number or to divine beings), Israel is YHWH's direct portion. The contrast emphasizes Israel's unique relationship with God.

---

## Key Phrase Analysis

### "Sons of Adam" (בְּנֵי אָדָם)

**Deuteronomy 32:8:**

> "when he separated the **sons of Adam** [בְּנֵי אָדָם]..."

**Meaning:** All humanity - the descendants of Adam. This is contrasted with "children of Israel" (or "sons of God" in DSS) in the second half of the verse.

**The Structure:**

| First Half | Second Half |
|------------|-------------|
| Divided nations | Set boundaries |
| Separated sons of Adam | According to number of [Israel/divine beings] |
| General humanity | Specific standard for division |

## Summary: Word Studies and Their Implications

### The Key Terms

| Hebrew | Meaning | Significance |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| עֶלְיוֹן (elyon) | Most High | GOD is the actor |
| נָחַל (nachal) | Divide/Inherit | Administrative action |
| פָּרַד (parad) | Separate | Geographic division |
| גְּבֻל (gebul) | Boundary | Territorial |
| חֵלֶק (cheleq) | Portion | Israel is YHWH's share |

### What the Words Describe

1. **God** (Most High) is the subject
2. The action is **dividing territories**
3. The result is **national boundaries**
4. Israel is God's **special portion**

### What the Words Do NOT Describe

- Marriage
- Reproduction
- Angels leaving heaven
- Sexual union
- Nephilim offspring

### The Critical Finding

Regardless of whether the original reading is "children of Israel" or "sons of God," the vocabulary describes:

| What Deut 32:8 Describes | What Genesis 6 Describes |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Territorial division | Taking wives |
| National boundaries | Bearing children |
| Administrative assignment | Domestic relationship |
| God as actor | "Sons of God" as actors |

**The terminology proves nothing about angels mating with humans.**
