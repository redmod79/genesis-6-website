# Topics Research: Deuteronomy 32:8 - "Sons of God" Textual Variant

## Question

The Dead Sea Scrolls (4QDeutj) read "sons of God" (בני אלהים) in Deuteronomy 32:8, while the Masoretic Text reads "children of Israel" (בני ישראל). Does this variant support the angel view of Genesis 6?

---

## The Textual Variant

### Three Main Readings

| Text | Hebrew/Greek | Translation |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Masoretic Text (MT)** | בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל (bene Yisrael) | "children of Israel" |
| **Dead Sea Scrolls (4QDeutj)** | בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים (bene elohim) | "sons of God" |
| **Septuagint (LXX)** | ἀγγέλων θεοῦ (angelōn theou) | "angels of God" |

### The Full Verse Comparison

**KJV (following MT):**
> "When the most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the **children of Israel**."

**DSS/LXX reading:**
> "...he set the bounds of the peoples according to the number of the **sons of God/angels of God**."

---

## Scholarly Debate

### Those Favoring DSS/LXX Reading

Some scholars argue:
- The MT may reflect a scribal alteration for theological reasons
- The DSS reading is older and more difficult (lectio difficilior)
- The 70 nations (Genesis 10) corresponding to 70 divine beings makes sense

### Those Favoring MT Reading

Others argue:
- The MT is well-attested and the received text
- "Children of Israel" parallels the 70 who went to Egypt (Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5)
- The DSS reading may reflect influence from pagan "divine council" concepts
- The change from בני ישראל to בני אלהים requires only adding letters

---

## The Key Question for This Study

**Even if the DSS reading is original, does this support the angel view of Genesis 6?**

The angel view argues:
1. DSS says nations were divided according to "sons of God" (divine beings)
2. This proves "sons of God" refers to angels/divine beings
3. Therefore "sons of God" in Genesis 6 means angels

**But this reasoning has problems:**

1. Deuteronomy 32:8 describes national boundaries - NOT mating with humans
2. Even if divine beings oversee nations, this doesn't mean they can reproduce
3. The verse says GOD divided the nations - the "sons of God" are passive
4. Job 1-2 uses "sons of God" for heavenly beings, but they don't marry

## Context of Deuteronomy 32

### The Song of Moses (Deuteronomy 32)

| Verses | Content |
|--------|---------|
| v.1-4 | Invocation - God is perfect, just, right |
| v.5-6 | Israel has corrupted themselves |
| v.7-9 | **God divided nations; Israel is His portion** |
| v.10-14 | God cared for Israel in the wilderness |
| v.15-18 | Israel forsook God for strange gods |
| v.19-25 | God's anger against Israel |
| v.26-43 | God's judgment and eventual vindication |

### The Immediate Context (v.8-9)

> v.8: "When the most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel."
> v.9: "For the LORD'S portion [is] his people; Jacob [is] the lot of his inheritance."

**The point:** While God assigned other nations their territories, He chose Israel as His special portion.

---

## The 70 Nations / 70 Israelites Connection

### Genesis 10 - Table of Nations

Genesis 10 lists the descendants of Noah's sons. Traditional Jewish counting identifies **70 nations**.

### Genesis 46:27 / Exodus 1:5

> **Exodus 1:5** - "And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were **seventy souls**"

**The MT reading makes sense:** God set national boundaries according to the number of Israelites (70) who would later go to Egypt. The 70 nations correspond to 70 Israelites - showing God's foreknowledge and plan for Israel.

---

## Deuteronomy 4:19-20 - Parallel Passage

> **Deuteronomy 4:19** - "And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven, and when thou seest the sun, and the moon, and the stars, [even] all the host of heaven, shouldest be driven to worship them, and serve them, which **the LORD thy God hath divided unto all nations** under the whole heaven."
> **Deuteronomy 4:20** - "But the LORD hath taken you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, [even] out of Egypt, **to be unto him a people of inheritance**, as [ye are] this day."

This passage:
- Says God "divided" heavenly bodies to nations (they worship them)
- Contrasts this with Israel as God's special inheritance
- Same theme as Deuteronomy 32:8-9

---

## Acts 17:26 - New Testament Parallel

> **Acts 17:26** - "And hath made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth, and hath **determined the times before appointed, and the bounds of their habitation**"

Paul attributes national boundaries to **GOD**, not to angels or divine beings.
