# Topics Research: Moses's Terminology for Humans with Special Relationship to God

## Question

How does Moses refer to humans who have a special relationship with God in the Pentateuch (Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy)?

## Purpose

To document what terminology Moses actually uses when referring to humans who have a special relationship with God - without making assumptions or analysis.

## Nave's Topical Bible - Relevant Topics

### SERVANT / SERVANTS OF GOD
- DEU 34:5 - Moses called "servant of the LORD"

### MAN OF GOD
- DEU 33:1 - Moses called "man of God"

### PROPHET / PROPHETESS
- GEN 20:7; EXO 7:1; 15:20; NUM 11:29; 12:6; DEU 13:1-5; 18:15-22; 34:10

### CHILDREN OF GOD
- DEU 14:1 - Israel called "children of the LORD your God"

### CHOSEN / ELECTION
- DEU 7:6; 14:2; 18:5 - Israel as chosen people

### HOLY PEOPLE / NATION
- EXO 19:6; DEU 7:6; 14:2,21; 26:19; 28:9

### PECULIAR PEOPLE / TREASURE
- EXO 19:5; DEU 14:2; 26:18

## Methodology

1. Search all Pentateuch occurrences of relationship terms
2. Document ONLY what Moses wrote - no interpretation
3. Present the terminology Moses used for humans in covenant relationship with God
