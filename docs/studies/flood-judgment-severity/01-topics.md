# Topics Research: Severity of the Flood Judgment

## Question

Why was the flood so severe? Does the worldwide destruction require something more than mere intermarriage (like genetic corruption) to explain it?

---

## Nave's Topical Dictionary Research

### FLOOD (The deluge)

> - Foretold GEN 6:13,17
> - History of GEN 6; 7; 8
> - References to JOB 22:16; PSA 90:5; MAT 24:38; LUK 17:26,27; HEB 11:7; 1PE 3:20; 2PE 2:5
> - The promise that it should not recur GEN 8:20,21; ISA 54:9

**Key observation:** The flood references in the NT (Matt 24, Luke 17, Heb 11, 1 Pet 3, 2 Pet 2) emphasize moral wickedness and judgment on "the ungodly," not genetic corruption.

### NOAH

> - Son of Lamech GEN 5:28,29
> - Builds an ark (ship) and saves his family from the great flood GEN 6:14-22; 7; 8; MAT 24:38; LUK 17:27; HEB 11:7; 1PE 3:20
> - Builds an altar and offers sacrifices GEN 8:20,21

**Key observation:** Noah is called "a preacher of righteousness" (2 Pet 2:5) - emphasizing his moral role, not his genetics.

### WICKED (PEOPLE)

> - God is angry with PSA 5:5,6; 7:11; ROM 9:13; 1CO 10:5
> - Spirit of God withdrawn from GEN 6:3; HOS 4:17-19; ROM 1:24,26,28
> - PUNISHMENT OF GEN 2:17; 3:16-19; 4:7; **6:3,7,12,13**...

**Key observation:** Genesis 6:3 is listed under "Spirit of God withdrawn from" the wicked - God's response to moral rebellion, not genetic corruption.

### JUDGMENT (The General)

> - According to opportunity and works GEN 4:7; JOB 34:11; PRO 11:31; 12:14; 24:11,12; PSA 62:12; ISA 3:10,11...
> - The flood references ISA 28:17,18; JER 25:31...

**Key observation:** Judgment is according to moral works and deeds, not genetics or bloodlines.

### CORRUPTION (Entry from Nave's)

> - FIGURATIVE of sin ISA 38:17; ROM 8:21; GAL 6:8; 2PE 1:4; 2:12,19
> - Mount of 2KI 23:13

**Key observation:** Nave's categorizes "corruption" as **figurative of sin** - moral/spiritual, not biological.

## Selected Topics for This Study

### Topic 1: God's Stated Reasons for the Flood

The text explicitly states why God sent the flood:

| Verse | Reason Given | Category |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| Gen 6:5 | "Wickedness of man was great" | Moral |
| Gen 6:5 | "Every imagination only evil continually" | Moral |
| Gen 6:11 | "Earth was corrupt before God" | Moral |
| Gen 6:11 | "Earth was filled with violence" | Moral |
| Gen 6:12 | "All flesh had corrupted his way" | Moral |
| Gen 6:13 | "Earth is filled with violence" | Moral |

**Critical finding:** Every stated reason is moral, not genetic.

### Topic 2: Comparison with Other Intermarriage Judgments

| Situation | Scripture | Judgment | Extent |
|-----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Pre-flood | Gen 6 | Worldwide flood | Universal ("all flesh") |
| Baal Peor | Num 25:1-9 | 24,000 died | Localized |
| Canaanites | Judg 3:5-8 | 8 years bondage | National |
| Solomon | 1 Kings 11 | Kingdom divided | Individual |
| Post-exile | Ezra 10 | Divorce required | Community |

**Key observation:** The difference is not the **type** of sin but the **extent** of corruption.

### Topic 3: The "Genetic Corruption" Claim

The angel view claims:
- The flood was necessary to purify the human gene pool
- Noah was "perfect in his generations" = genetically pure
- Only genetic corruption explains the flood's severity

**This study examines:** Do the Hebrew words support moral or genetic corruption?
