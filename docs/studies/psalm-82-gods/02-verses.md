# Verses: Psalm 82:6 - "Ye Are Gods"

## Primary Text - Psalm 82 (Complete)

> **Psalm 82:1** A Psalm of Asaph. God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the **gods**.

> **Psalm 82:2** How long will ye **judge unjustly**, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah.

> **Psalm 82:3** **Defend the poor and fatherless**: do justice to the afflicted and needy.

> **Psalm 82:4** **Deliver the poor and needy**: rid [them] out of the hand of the wicked.

> **Psalm 82:5** They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are out of course.

> **Psalm 82:6** I have said, **Ye [are] gods**; and all of you [are] **children of the most High**.

> **Psalm 82:7** But ye shall **die like men**, and fall like one of the princes.

> **Psalm 82:8** Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.

**Observations:**
- v.1: God judges "among the gods" - the assembly
- v.2-4: **Judicial duties** - judging, defending poor, delivering needy
- v.5: Human ignorance - "they know not"
- v.6: Honorific title - "gods," "children of the most High"
- v.7: Mortality - "die like men," "fall like one of the princes"
- v.8: True God will judge the earth

## Jesus's Interpretation - John 10:34-36

> **John 10:33** The Jews answered him, saying, For a good work we stone thee not; but for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, makest thyself God.

> **John 10:34** Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, **I said, Ye are gods**?

> **John 10:35** If he called them **gods, unto whom the word of God came**, and the scripture cannot be broken;

> **John 10:36** Say ye of him, whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest; because I said, I am the Son of God?

**Jesus's identification:** The "gods" were those "unto whom the word of God came" - i.e., **Israel receiving the Law**.

## "Elohim" Translated as "Judges" - Exodus 21-22

### Exodus 21:6

> **Exodus 21:6** Then his master shall bring him unto the **judges** [הָאֱלֹהִים, ha'elohim]; he shall also bring him to the door, or unto the door post; and his master shall bore his ear through with an aul; and he shall serve him for ever.

### Exodus 22:8

> **Exodus 22:8** If the thief be not found, then the master of the house shall be brought unto the **judges** [הָאֱלֹהִים, ha'elohim], [to see] whether he have put his hand unto his neighbour's goods.

### Exodus 22:9

> **Exodus 22:9** For all manner of trespass, [whether it be] for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, [or] for any manner of lost thing, which [another] challengeth to be his, the cause of both parties shall come before the **judges** [הָאֱלֹהִים, ha'elohim]; [and] whom the **judges** [אֱלֹהִים, elohim] shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.

### Exodus 22:28

> **Exodus 22:28** Thou shalt not revile the **gods** [אֱלֹהִים, elohim], nor curse the ruler of thy people.

**Note:** The KJV margin reads "judges" for "gods" here. The parallel with "ruler of thy people" confirms human authorities are in view.

**Key finding:** The same Hebrew word אֱלֹהִים (elohim) used in Psalm 82:6 is translated "judges" in these passages - referring to HUMAN authorities.

## Parallel Psalm - Psalm 58 (Corrupt Judges)

> **Psalm 58:1** To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David. Do ye indeed speak righteousness, O congregation? do ye **judge** uprightly, O ye **sons of men**?

> **Psalm 58:2** Yea, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh the violence of your hands in the earth.

> **Psalm 58:11** So that a man shall say, Verily [there is] a reward for the righteous: verily he is a **God that judgeth in the earth**.

**Observations:**
- Same theme: corrupt judges failing to judge righteously
- Explicitly calls them "**sons of men**" (בְּנֵי אָדָם, bene adam)
- Ends with affirmation that God judges in the earth (cf. Psalm 82:8)

## "Like Men" in Other Contexts

### Hosea 6:7

> **Hosea 6:7** But they **like men** [כְּאָדָם, ke'adam] have transgressed the covenant: there have they dealt treacherously against me.

**Note:** Israel transgressed "like men" - but they WERE men. The phrase doesn't require non-human origin.

### 1 Samuel 4:9

> **1 Samuel 4:9** Be strong, and quit yourselves **like men**, O ye Philistines, that ye be not servants unto the Hebrews, as they have been to you: quit yourselves **like men**, and fight.

**Note:** Philistines told to act "like men" - they were men. The phrase means "act as men should act."

## "Children of God" Applied to Humans

### Matthew 5:9

> **Matthew 5:9** Blessed [are] the peacemakers: for they shall be called the **children of God**.

### John 1:12

> **John 1:12** But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the **sons of God**, [even] to them that believe on his name

### Romans 8:14

> **Romans 8:14** For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the **sons of God**.

### Galatians 3:26

> **Galatians 3:26** For ye are all the **children of God** by faith in Christ Jesus.

### 1 John 3:1-2

> **1 John 3:1** Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the **sons of God**...
> **1 John 3:2** Beloved, now are we the **sons of God**...

**Key finding:** "Children/sons of God" is used throughout Scripture for BELIEVERS - humans who have a special relationship with God.

## Summary of Verse Evidence

| Category | Passages | Finding |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Psalm 82 context | v.2-4 | **Judicial duties** - human activity |
| Jesus's interpretation | John 10:34-35 | "gods" = those who received God's word (humans) |
| Elohim = judges | Exodus 21:6; 22:8, 9, 28 | Human authorities called "elohim" |
| Parallel psalm | Psalm 58:1 | Same theme, calls them "**sons of men**" |
| "Like men" usage | Hosea 6:7; 1 Sam 4:9 | Phrase used of those who ARE men |
| "Children of God" | Matt 5:9; John 1:12; Rom 8:14; Gal 3:26; 1 John 3:1-2 | Applied to human believers |

**Every line of evidence points to HUMAN judges, not divine beings.**
