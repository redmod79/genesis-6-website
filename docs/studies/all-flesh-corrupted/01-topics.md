# Topics Research: "All Flesh Had Corrupted His Way" (Genesis 6:12)

## Question

The angel view argues that "all flesh had corrupted his way" implies more than moral corruption - suggesting genetic/biological contamination through angel-human hybridization that required the flood to purify the corrupted gene pool.

Does this phrase support the genetic corruption interpretation?

---

## Nave's Topical Dictionary Research

### FLESH

> - FIGURATIVE
>   - Fruits of GAL 5:19-21
> - SYMBOLICAL
>   - Body of Christ symbolized by the bread JHN 6:51-63

**Key observation from Nave's:** "Flesh" in Scripture is used literally for physical bodies and figuratively for the sinful nature. Galatians 5:19-21 lists "works of the flesh" - all MORAL sins (adultery, fornication, idolatry, hatred, wrath, etc.), not genetic conditions.

### CORRUPTION

> - PHYSICAL DECOMPOSITION LEV 22:25
>   - After death GEN 3:19; JOB 17:14; PSA 16:10; ACT 2:27,31; 1CO 15:42,50
> - FIGURATIVE
>   - **Of sin ISA 38:17; ROM 8:21; GAL 6:8; 2PE 1:4; 2:12,19**
>   - Mount of 2KI 23:13

**Key observation:** Nave's categorizes corruption as either:
1. Physical decomposition (dead bodies rotting)
2. Figurative - **of sin** (moral corruption)

There is no category for "genetic corruption."

### WAY (Figurative)

> - FIGURATIVE
>   - Of holiness PSA 16:11; ISA 35:8,9; JER 6:16; HOS 14:9
>   - Of righteousness, narrow MAT 7:14
>   - Of sin, broad MAT 7:13
>   - Jesus is the JHN 14:6; HEB 9:8
>   - Doctrines taught by Christ ACT 9:2; 19:23; 22:4; 24:14,22

**Key observation:** "Way" in Scripture is used figuratively for:
- Holiness, righteousness (moral)
- Sin (moral)
- Doctrines/teaching (religious)

Never for genetics or biology.

| Passage | Text | Context |
|---------|------|---------|

| **Deuteronomy 31:29** | "corrupt yourselves, turn aside from **the way** which I have commanded" | Moses's warning |
| **Judges 2:19** | "corrupted themselves more than their fathers...ceased not from their stubborn **way**" | Post-Joshua apostasy |
| **Ezekiel 16:47** | "corrupted more than they in all thy **ways**" | Jerusalem's sins |
| **Malachi 2:8** | "departed out of **the way**...corrupted the covenant" | Corrupt priests |

**Every instance = MORAL corruption.** Never genetic.

## Selected Topics for This Study

### Topic 1: What "Corrupt His Way" Means

Hebrew: שָׁחַת (shachath) + דֶּרֶךְ (derek)

- **Shachath** = to ruin, spoil, act corruptly (morally)
- **Derek** = way, path, manner of life, conduct

"Corrupt his way" = ruined his manner of life, moral conduct.

### Topic 2: The Parallel Passages

Every other use of "corrupt + way" in Scripture describes MORAL corruption:
- Idolatry (golden calf)
- Religious apostasy (Judges)
- Covenant violation (Malachi)

### Topic 3: Context of Genesis 6:12

The immediate context gives the meaning:

| Verse | Description | Category |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 6:5 | "wickedness...every imagination...only evil" | **Moral** |
| 6:11 | "corrupt before God...filled with violence" | **Moral** |
| 6:12 | "all flesh had corrupted his way" | **Moral** |
| 6:13 | "filled with violence" | **Moral** |

The phrase is surrounded by moral descriptions.
