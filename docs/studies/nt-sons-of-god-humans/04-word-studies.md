# Word Studies

## huios (G5207) - Son

**Original:** υἱός
**Transliteration:** huios
**Pronunciation:** hwee-os'
**Definition:** A son; used of immediate, remote, or figurative kinship. Apparently a primary word.

### Meaning and Usage
The Greek word huios emphasizes the **inward, ethical, and legal aspects** of relationship. It denotes:
1. Literal male offspring
2. Descendants/posterity (e.g., "sons of Abraham")
3. Those who belong to or are characterized by something (e.g., "sons of disobedience")
4. Those in a special relationship (e.g., "Son of God" for Christ, believers)

### Usage in Key Verses
- **John 1:12** - "sons of God" (huious theou) - those who receive Christ
- **Romans 8:14** - "sons of God" (huioi theou) - those led by the Spirit
- **Romans 8:19** - "sons of God" (huion tou theou) - awaiting manifestation
- **Philippians 2:15** - "sons of God" (tekna theou in some MSS, but huioi in TR)
- **1 John 3:1, 2** - "sons of God" (tekna theou) - present reality

### Significance for This Study
When applied to believers, huios emphasizes:
- Legal standing (adoption rights)
- Ethical character (reflecting the Father)
- Inheritance rights (heirs of God)

## teknon (G5043) - Child

**Original:** τέκνον
**Transliteration:** teknon
**Pronunciation:** tek'-non
**Definition:** A child (as produced); offspring. From the base of G5088 (tikto - to bring forth).

### Meaning and Usage
The Greek word teknon emphasizes the **physical and outward aspects** of parentage. It denotes:
1. Literal offspring
2. Pupils or disciples (as "children" of their teachers)
3. Those in intimate relationship formed through bonds of love and trust

### Metaphorical Usage
- "Children of God" (tekna theou) - believers led by God's Spirit
- "Children of the devil" - those characterized by evil
- "Children of wisdom" - those nurtured by wisdom
- "Children of wrath" - those under God's judgment

### Usage in Key Verses
- **John 1:12** - "children of God" (tekna theou) - power to become
- **Romans 8:16, 17, 21** - "children of God" (tekna theou) - Spirit's witness
- **Galatians 3:26** - "children of God" (huioi theou, though KJV translates "children")
- **1 John 3:1, 2, 10** - "children of God" (tekna theou) - present status

### Distinction from huios
| Aspect | huios (son) | teknon (child) |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Emphasis | Legal, ethical, inward | Physical, relational, outward |
| Focus | Status, rights, character | Origin, birth, nurture |
| Inheritance | Stressed | Implied |

Both terms are used interchangeably in some contexts (e.g., Romans 8:14 uses huioi, v.16-17 uses tekna), suggesting they overlap significantly when referring to believers.

## huiothesia (G5206) - Adoption

**Original:** υἱοθεσία
**Transliteration:** huiothesia
**Pronunciation:** hwee-oth-es-ee'-ah
**Definition:** The placing as a son; adoption (figuratively, Christian sonship in respect to God).

### Etymology
Compound word:
- υἱός (huios) = son
- θέσις (thesis, from τίθημι/tithemi) = placing, putting

Literally: "the placing as a son"

### NT Occurrences (5 total)
1. **Romans 8:15** - "Spirit of adoption" (pneuma huiothesias)
2. **Romans 8:23** - "waiting for the adoption" (huiothesian apekdechomenoi)
3. **Romans 9:4** - Israel's adoption (he huiothesia)
4. **Galatians 4:5** - "receive the adoption of sons" (huiothesian apolabomen)
5. **Ephesians 1:5** - "adoption of children" (huiothesian)

### Three Applications in NT

**1. Israel's Covenant Status (Rom 9:4)**
God's special relationship with Israel as His "son" (cf. Exodus 4:22-23).

**2. Present Christian Identity (Rom 8:15; Gal 4:5; Eph 1:5)**
Believers receive adoption NOW through Christ. Evidence:
- Spirit of adoption received
- Cry "Abba, Father"
- No longer servants but sons

**3. Future Fulfillment (Rom 8:23)**
"Waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body"
- Full adoption awaits bodily transformation
- Present adoption is "firstfruits"
- Complete adoption includes resurrection

### Significance for This Study
Huiothesia reveals that adoption has TWO PHASES:
1. **Present**: Received through faith, evidenced by Spirit
2. **Future**: Completed at resurrection ("redemption of our body")

Romans 8:23 is crucial: believers have "firstfruits of the Spirit" (present) but still await "the adoption, the redemption of our body" (future). The same word (huiothesia) is used for both what they've received (v.15) and what they await (v.23).

## isangelos (Implied in Luke 20:36)

**Original:** ἰσάγγελος
**Transliteration:** isangelos
**Pronunciation:** ee-sang'-el-os
**Definition:** Like an angel, angel-like.

### Etymology
Compound word:
- ἴσος (isos) = equal
- ἄγγελος (angelos) = angel, messenger

### Usage in Luke 20:36
"Neither can they die any more: for they are equal unto the angels (isangeloi); and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection."

### Significance for This Study
This describes the FUTURE state of believers after resurrection:
- Cannot die anymore (immortal)
- Equal to/like angels (transformed nature)
- "Children of God" linked to "children of the resurrection"

This confirms that full sonship involves physical transformation to an immortal, angel-like state.

## Summary: Word Studies and the Question

The Greek terminology confirms the "now and not yet" pattern:

| Word | Present Aspect | Future Aspect |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| huios/teknon | Believers ARE sons/children now | Full manifestation awaited |
| huiothesia | Spirit of adoption received | Full adoption = bodily redemption |
| isangelos | Not applicable | Describes resurrection state |

The same word (huiothesia) is used for both present reception and future completion, demonstrating that adoption is a process with distinct phases, not a single event.
