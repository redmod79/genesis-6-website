# Topics Research

## Question
Does "sons of God" or "children of God" in the NT refer to humans? If so, when - on earth or after translation to heaven? Does it require a change in their being/humanity/physical form?

## Semantic Search Results
Topics identified through search for "sons of God", "children of God", "adoption":

1. ADOPTION - Primary topic on spiritual sonship
2. CHILDREN - Contains sections on children of God
3. CALL - Personal calling to sonship
4. BENEDICTIONS - Reference to sons of God in Rev 21:7
5. WAR (Figurative) - Reference to sons of God in Rev 21:7
