# Word Studies: Genesis 6 Key Terms

## Key Term 1: "Sons of God" (בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים - bene ha'elohim)

### Component Words

**בֵּן (ben)** - Strong's H1121
- **Transliteration:** ben
- **Pronunciation:** bane
- **Definition:** son, child, descendant
- **Root:** From בָּנָה (banah, H1129) = "to build"
- **Usage:** 4,906 times in KJV (son 2,978x; children 1,568x)

**אֱלֹהִים (elohim)** - Strong's H430
- **Transliteration:** elohim
- **Pronunciation:** el-o-heem
- **Definition:** God, gods, divine beings, judges
- **Usage:** 2,606 times in KJV (God 2,346x; god 244x)
- **Note:** Plural form, used for the true God and for false gods

### The Full Phrase: בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים (bene ha'elohim)

**Literal:** "sons of the God" or "sons of God"

### Old Testament Occurrences

| Reference | Hebrew | Translation | Context |
|-----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| Gen 6:2 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | sons of God | Taking wives |
| Gen 6:4 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | sons of God | Coming unto daughters of men |
| Job 1:6 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | sons of God | Presenting before the LORD |
| Job 2:1 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | sons of God | Presenting before the LORD |
| Job 38:7 | בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים | sons of God | At creation, shouting for joy |

**Observation:** The exact phrase "bene ha'elohim" appears only 5 times in the Hebrew Bible - twice in Genesis 6 and three times in Job.

### Related Phrases

**בְּנֵי אֵלִים (bene elim)** - "sons of the mighty" or "sons of gods"
- Psalm 29:1: "Give unto the LORD, O ye mighty [bene elim]..."
- Psalm 89:6: "...who among the sons of the mighty [bene elim] can be likened unto the LORD?"

**בְּנֵי עֶלְיוֹן (bene elyon)** - "sons of the Most High"
- Psalm 82:6: "I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children [bene] of the most High [elyon]."

**בָּנִים לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם (banim la'YHWH elohekem)** - "children to the LORD your God"
- Deuteronomy 14:1: "Ye are the children of the LORD your God..."

## Key Term 2: "Daughters of Men" (בְּנוֹת הָאָדָם - benoth ha'adam)

### Component Words

**בַּת (bath)** - Strong's H1323
- **Transliteration:** bath
- **Pronunciation:** bath
- **Definition:** daughter
- **Usage:** 587 times in KJV

**אָדָם (adam)** - Strong's H120
- **Transliteration:** adam
- **Pronunciation:** aw-dawm
- **Definition:** man, mankind, human being
- **Root:** From H119, related to אֲדָמָה (adamah) = ground, earth
- **Usage:** 552 times in KJV (man 408x; Adam 13x)

### The Full Phrase

**בְּנוֹת הָאָדָם** = "daughters of THE man" or "daughters of mankind"

**Observation:** The phrase uses "adam" with the definite article, suggesting "the man" (Adam's descendants) or "mankind" generally.

## Key Term 3: "Giants" (נְפִלִים - nephilim)

### Definition

**נְפִלִים (nephilim)** - Strong's H5303
- **Transliteration:** nephilim
- **Pronunciation:** nef-eel-eem
- **Definition:** giants, fallen ones
- **Possible root:** נָפַל (naphal, H5307) = "to fall"

### All Biblical Occurrences

| Reference | Text |
|-----------|------|
| Gen 6:4 | "There were **giants** [nephilim] in the earth in those days; and also after that..." |
| Num 13:33 | "And there we saw the **giants** [nephilim], the sons of Anak, which come of the giants [nephilim]..." |

**Only 2 occurrences in the entire Bible.**

### Interpretation Options

1. **"Fallen ones"** - from naphal (to fall)
   - Could mean: those who fell (morally), those who cause others to fall, or offspring of fallen angels

2. **"Giants"** - physical stature
   - Septuagint (LXX) translates as γίγαντες (gigantes)
   - Numbers 13:33 clearly associates them with large stature

3. **"Mighty men"** - warriors/tyrants
   - Genesis 6:4 describes them as "mighty men... men of renown"
   - Could refer to powerful rulers/warriors

### Key Observation

Genesis 6:4 states: "There were giants in the earth in those days; **and also after that**..."

This means Nephilim existed:
- BEFORE the sons of God/daughters of men unions
- AFTER these unions
- After the flood (Numbers 13:33 - in Canaan)

## Key Term 4: "Flesh" (בָּשָׂר - basar)

### Definition

**בָּשָׂר (basar)** - Strong's H1320
- **Transliteration:** basar
- **Pronunciation:** baw-sawr
- **Definition:** flesh, body, mankind
- **Usage:** 269 times in KJV

### Usage in Genesis 6:3

> "My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that **he also is flesh**..."

**Hebrew:** בְּשַׁגַּם הוּא בָשָׂר (beshagam hu basar)

**Literal:** "in that also he [is] flesh"

**Key question:** Who is "he" that is "also flesh"?
- If referring to man generally - humans are flesh
- If referring to the "sons of God" - they too are flesh (human)

**Observation:** Angels are called "spirits" (Heb 1:14), not "flesh." If the "sons of God" were angels, why does God call them "flesh"?

## Key Term 5: "Took" (לָקַח - laqach)

### Definition

**לָקַח (laqach)** - Strong's H3947
- **Transliteration:** laqach
- **Pronunciation:** law-kakh
- **Definition:** to take, get, fetch, lay hold of, seize, receive, acquire, buy, bring, marry
- **Usage:** 965 times in KJV

### Usage in Genesis 6:2

> "...and they **took** them wives of all which they chose."

**Observation:** This is standard vocabulary for marriage. Compare:
- Gen 4:19: "And Lamech **took** unto him two wives..."
- Gen 11:29: "And Abram and Nahor **took** them wives..."
- Gen 24:67: "And Isaac... **took** Rebekah, and she became his wife..."

The vocabulary describes normal human marriage, not abduction or supernatural union.

## Summary: What Word Studies Reveal

| Term | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **bene ha'elohim** | Used for heavenly beings in Job; used in Genesis 6 in context of human affairs |
| **benoth ha'adam** | Daughters of mankind/humanity |
| **nephilim** | Existed before AND after the unions; also post-flood in Canaan |
| **basar (flesh)** | God calls "he" (the sons of God?) flesh - not typical for angels |
| **laqach (took)** | Standard marriage vocabulary used throughout Genesis |

### Critical Observations

1. **The phrase "bene elohim" is rare** - only 5 OT occurrences
2. **Context differs** - Job describes heavenly scenes; Genesis describes human affairs
3. **God's response focuses on "flesh"** - unusual if the problem was angels
4. **Nephilim problem** - they existed before, during, and after the events, and post-flood
5. **Marriage language is normal** - no indication of supernatural union
