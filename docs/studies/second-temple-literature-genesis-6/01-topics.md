# Nave's Topical Research: Second Temple Literature and Genesis 6

## Topics Searched

### ENOCH
**Nave's Entry:**
- 1. Oldest son of Cain (Gen 4:17,18)
- 2. A city built by Cain (Gen 4:17)
- 3. Father of Methuselah. Transporting of (Gen 5:18-24; Luk 3:37; Heb 11:5; Jude 1:14). Called HENOCH (1Ch 1:3)

**Key Observations:**
- Nave's lists Jude 1:14 as a reference for Enoch the person, not for 1 Enoch the book
- The entry connects Enoch to translation/walking with God, not to angelic narratives
- Scripture's portrait of Enoch: walked with God, was translated, prophesied judgment

### ANTEDILUVIANS
**Nave's Entry:**
- Worship God (Gen 4:3,4,26)
- Occupations of (Gen 4:2,3,20-22)
- Arts of (Gen 4:2,3,20-22; 6:14-22)
- **Enoch prophesies to** (Jude 1:14,15)
- Noah preaches to (Heb 11:7; 1Pe 3:18-20; 2Pe 2:5)
- Wickedness of (Gen 6:5-7)
- Destruction of (Gen 7:1,21-23; Job 22:15-17; Mat 24:37-39; Luk 17:26,27; 2Pe 2:5)
- Giants among -- See GIANTS
- Longevity of -- See LONGEVITY

**Key Observations:**
- Nave's attributes Jude 1:14-15 to the person Enoch prophesying to antediluvians
- The entry treats the antediluvian period as a time of human wickedness, arts, and occupations
- No mention of angelic invasion or supernatural hybridization
- Giants are listed separately, not as angelic offspring

### GIANTS
**Nave's Entry:**
- General scriptures concerning (Gen 6:4)
- See ANAKIM, EMIMS, GOLIATH, ISHBI-BENOB, OG, REPHAIM, ZAMZUMMIM

**Key Observations:**
- The only "general scripture" for giants is Gen 6:4
- All specific giant references are to known human ethnic groups (Anakim, Rephaim, Emims, Zamzummims)
- No mention of angelic parentage or supernatural origin
- Giants are catalogued as races and individuals, not as angel-human hybrids

### ANGELS
**Nave's Entry (relevant sections):**
- Legion of angels (Mat 26:53)
- Entertains three angels (Gen 18:1-15) -- Abraham at Mamre
- Election of good angels (1Ti 5:21)
- Jacob's vision of angels (Gen 28:12; Gen 32:2)
- Angels announced births, delivered messages, executed judgment

**Key Observations:**
- Nave's extensive angel entry does NOT list Genesis 6 under angel references
- Angels in Scripture function as messengers, executors of judgment, worshippers
- No entry links angels to marriage, reproduction, or hybrid offspring

### PROPHECY
**Nave's Entry (relevant selections):**
- A mode of divine communication
- Prophets spoke under inspiration
- Tests of prophets: fulfillment of predictions, alignment with Torah
- Enoch's prophecy (Jude 1:14-15) listed under ANTEDILUVIANS

**Key Observations:**
- Prophecy in Scripture is about divine communication through human vessels
- Paul calls Epimenides "a prophet of their own" (Titus 1:12) -- using prophetes for a pagan poet
- The term "prophesied" (propheteuo) can denote speaking truth, not necessarily canonical endorsement

---

## Summary of Topical Findings

1. **Nave's does not connect Genesis 6 to angels** -- The GIANTS topic references Gen 6:4 but lists only human giant races
2. **Enoch is treated as a historical person who prophesied** -- Not as the author of 1 Enoch the book
3. **The antediluvian narrative focuses on human wickedness** -- Arts, occupations, worship, moral corruption
4. **Angels in Nave's are messengers and ministers** -- No references to reproduction or intermarriage
5. **Prophecy involves human vessels speaking divine truth** -- The term can be applied broadly (Titus 1:12)
