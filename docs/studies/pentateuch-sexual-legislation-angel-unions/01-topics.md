## Key Observation

**Every category of sexual sin known to the ancient world is addressed in the Pentateuch legislation.** The categories covered include:

1. Adultery (Lev 18:20; 20:10)
2. Incest - 13 specific relationships listed (Lev 18:6-18)
3. Homosexuality (Lev 18:22; 20:13)
4. Bestiality (Lev 18:23; 20:15-16; Exo 22:19)
5. Prostitution / Harlotry (Lev 19:29; Deu 23:17-18)
6. Rape (Deu 22:25-27)
7. Premarital sex (Deu 22:13-21; Exo 22:16-17)
8. Intermarriage with pagans (Deu 7:3-4; Exo 34:15-16)
9. Sex during menstruation (Lev 18:19; 20:18)
10. Levirate marriage obligations (Deu 25:5-10)
11. Priestly marriage restrictions (Lev 21:7,13-15)
12. Child sacrifice (associated with sexual rites, Lev 18:21; 20:2-5)

**What is NOT addressed:** Angel-human sexual contact.

Despite the comprehensiveness of this legislation -- covering even edge cases like Deuteronomy 25:11-12 (a woman grabbing a man's genitals during a fight) -- there is zero mention of angel-human unions, warnings about celestial beings having sexual contact with humans, or any acknowledgment that such a thing is possible.
