# Nave's Topical Research: Jesus on Angels and Marriage

## Topics Searched

### ANGEL (A Spirit)
*From Nave's Topical Bible*

**Key entries relevant to this study:**

- **Do not marry:** MAT 22:30; MRK 12:25; LUK 20:35
- **Immortal:** LUK 20:36
- **Are spirits:** HEB 1:7, 14
- **Called MORNING STARS:** JOB 38:7
- **Called PRINCIPALITIES, POWERS:** EPH 3:10; COL 1:16
- **Of different orders:** ISA 6:2; 1TH 4:16; 1PE 3:22; Jude 1:9; REV 12:7
- **Are obedient:** PSA 103:20; MAT 6:10; LUK 11:2; 1PE 3:22; 2PE 2:11; Jude 1:6
- **Mighty:** PSA 103:20; 2PE 2:11
- **Holy:** MAT 25:31; MRK 8:38
- **Elect:** 1TI 5:21
- **FALLEN** (category noted but no separate entries provided)

**Observation:** Nave's lists "Do not marry" as a categorical attribute of angels alongside "Immortal," "Are spirits," "Mighty," "Holy," etc. The topical index treats non-marriage as a characteristic of angelic NATURE, not merely a behavioral choice of some angels.

### MARRIAGE
*From Nave's Topical Bible*

**Key entries relevant to this study:**

- **Not binding after death:** MAT 22:29,30; MRK 12:24,25
- **Levirate (the brother required to marry a brother's widow):** GEN 38:8,11; DEU 25:5-10; RUT 4:5; MAT 22:24; MRK 12:19-23; LUK 20:28
- **Figurative:** ISA 54:5; 62:4,5; JER 3:14; 31:32; HOS 1:2; 2:19,20; EPH 5:30-32; REV 19:7-9
- **Parables from:** MAT 22:2; 25:1-10

**Observation:** The "not binding after death" entry directly connects to our primary passages. The levirate marriage context is important because this is what the Sadducees' question was about.

### RESURRECTION
*From Nave's Topical Bible*

**Key entries relevant to this study:**

- **General scriptures:** MAT 22:23-32; MRK 12:18-27; LUK 14:14; 20:27-38; JHN 5:21,25,28,29; 6:39,40,44,54; 11:23-25; ACT 23:6,8; 24:14,15; ROM 8:10,11,19,21-23; 1CO 6:14; 15:12-57; PHP 3:10,11,21; 1TH 4:14,16
- **Typified:** Isaac (GEN 22:13 with HEB 11:19); Jonah (JON 2:10 with MAT 12:40)

**Observation:** All three Synoptic accounts are listed as primary resurrection texts. The Sadducees' denial of resurrection was the context for Jesus's teaching about angels and marriage.

---

## Cross-Reference Pattern

The three topics converge at the same texts:
- **ANGEL** lists MAT 22:30, MRK 12:25, LUK 20:35 under "Do not marry"
- **MARRIAGE** lists MAT 22:29-30, MRK 12:24-25 under "Not binding after death"
- **RESURRECTION** lists all three Synoptic pericopes as general resurrection scriptures

This convergence confirms that Jesus's teaching sits at the intersection of angel nature, marriage institution, and resurrection doctrine. It is not peripheral to any of these topics -- it is a defining text for all three.
