# Verses: Septuagint Translation of Genesis 6:2

## Primary Verses - Genesis 6 (Hebrew Original → KJV)

### Genesis 6:1-4

> **Genesis 6:1** And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them,

> **Genesis 6:2** That the **sons of God** saw the daughters of men that they [were] fair; and they **took them wives** of all which they chose.

> **Genesis 6:3** And the LORD said, My spirit shall not always strive with **man**, for that **he also is flesh**: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

> **Genesis 6:4** There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the **sons of God** came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare [children] to them, the same [became] mighty men which [were] of old, men of renown.

## Comparison Verses - Job (Same Hebrew Phrase)

### Job 1:6

> **Job 1:6** Now there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan** came also **among them**.

**Context:** Heavenly throne-room scene. Satan is present among the "sons of God."

### Job 2:1

> **Job 2:1** Again there was a day when the **sons of God** came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan** came also among them to present himself before the LORD.

**Context:** Same heavenly throne-room scene repeated.

### Job 38:7

> **Job 38:7** When the morning stars sang together, and all the **sons of God** shouted for joy?

**Context:** Creation account - this occurred before humans existed. Clearly refers to celestial beings.

## The Hebrew Original

All five occurrences use the same phrase:

| Reference | Hebrew | Transliteration |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Genesis 6:2 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | bene ha'elohim |
| Genesis 6:4 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | bene ha'elohim |
| Job 1:6 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | bene ha'elohim |
| Job 2:1 | בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים | bene ha'elohim |
| Job 38:7 | בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים | bene elohim |

**The Hebrew is essentially identical in all cases.**

## The Septuagint (LXX) Translations

### Genesis 6:2 (LXX)

**Greek:** ἰδόντες δὲ οἱ **υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ** τὰς θυγατέρας τῶν ἀνθρώπων...

**Translation:** "And the **sons of God** (huioi tou theou) saw the daughters of men..."

### Genesis 6:4 (LXX)

**Greek:** ...οἱ **υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ** εἰσεπορεύοντο πρὸς τὰς θυγατέρας τῶν ἀνθρώπων...

**Translation:** "...the **sons of God** (huioi tou theou) came in unto the daughters of men..."

### Job 1:6 (LXX)

**Greek:** καὶ ὡς ἐγένετο ἡ ἡμέρα αὕτη καὶ ἰδοὺ ἦλθον οἱ **ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ** παραστῆναι ἐνώπιον τοῦ κυρίου

**Translation:** "And it came to pass on this day, behold the **angels of God** (angeloi tou theou) came to stand before the Lord..."

### Job 2:1 (LXX)

**Greek:** ἦλθον οἱ **ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ** παραστῆναι ἔναντι κυρίου

**Translation:** "The **angels of God** (angeloi tou theou) came to stand before the Lord..."

### Job 38:7 (LXX)

**Greek:** ὅτε ἐγενήθησαν ἄστρα ᾔνεσάν με φωνῇ μεγάλῃ πάντες **ἄγγελοί μου**

**Translation:** "When the stars were made, all **my angels** (angeloi mou) praised me with a loud voice..."

## Supporting Verse - Moses's Use of "Children of God"

### Deuteronomy 14:1

> **Deuteronomy 14:1** Ye [are] the **children of the LORD your God**: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.

**Hebrew:** בָּנִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם (banim attem la'YHWH elohekem)

**Significance:** Moses calls Israel "children of the LORD your God" - demonstrating that Moses used "sons/children of God" language for humans, not just celestial beings.

---

## Summary of LXX Translation Choices

| Reference | Hebrew | LXX Greek | LXX Translation | Context |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| Gen 6:2 | bene ha'elohim | υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ | **sons of God** | Earthly; marriage |
| Gen 6:4 | bene ha'elohim | υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ | **sons of God** | Earthly; marriage |
| Job 1:6 | bene ha'elohim | ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ | **angels of God** | Heavenly throne room |
| Job 2:1 | bene ha'elohim | ἄγγελοι τοῦ θεοῦ | **angels of God** | Heavenly throne room |
| Job 38:7 | bene elohim | ἄγγελοί μου | **my angels** | Creation; before humans |

**Key Finding:** The LXX translators chose different Greek words based on context, even though the Hebrew was the same.
