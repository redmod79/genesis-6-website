# Verses: Jude 1:6-7 "In Like Manner" Study

## Primary Passage: Jude 1:4-9

**Jude 1:4** For there are certain men crept in unawares, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation, ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness, and denying the only Lord God, and our Lord Jesus Christ.

**Jude 1:5** I will therefore put you in remembrance, though ye once knew this, how that the Lord, having saved the people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed them that believed not.

**Jude 1:6** And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.

**Jude 1:7** Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.

**Jude 1:8** Likewise also these filthy dreamers defile the flesh, despise dominion, and speak evil of dignities.

**Jude 1:9** Yet Michael the archangel, when contending with the devil he disputed about the body of Moses, durst not bring against him a railing accusation, but said, The Lord rebuke thee.

---

## Parallel Passage: 2 Peter 2:4-10

**2 Peter 2:4** For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment;

**2 Peter 2:5** And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

**2 Peter 2:6** And turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrha into ashes condemned them with an overthrow, making them an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly;

**2 Peter 2:7** And delivered just Lot, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked:

**2 Peter 2:8** (For that righteous man dwelling among them, in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul from day to day with their unlawful deeds;)

**2 Peter 2:9** The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished:

**2 Peter 2:10** But chiefly them that walk after the flesh in the lust of uncleanness, and despise government. Presumptuous are they, selfwilled, they are not afraid to speak evil of dignities.

### Matthew 8:34 (F → M Pattern)

**Matthew 8:34** And, behold, the whole city came out to meet Jesus: and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart out of their coasts.

*Greek: πᾶσα ἡ πόλις (F sg) → ἰδόντες (M pl) "seeing"*
*Pattern: The city (feminine) → "seeing" (masculine participle) - inhabitants in view*

### John 4:39 (F → M Pattern)

**John 4:39** And many of the Samaritans of that city believed on him for the saying of the woman, which testified, He told me all that ever I did.

*Greek: τῆς πόλεως ἐκείνης (F sg) → πολλοί (M pl) "many"*
*Pattern: "From that city" (feminine) → "many" (masculine) - inhabitants in view*

### Acts 21:30 (F → M Pattern)

**Acts 21:30** And all the city was moved, and the people ran together: and they took Paul, and drew him out of the temple: and forthwith the doors were shut.

*Greek: ἡ πόλις ὅλη (F sg) → ἐπιλαβόμενοι (M pl) "seizing"*
*Pattern: The city (feminine) → "seizing" (masculine participle) - inhabitants in view*

### Mark 1:33 (F → F Pattern - Strict Agreement)

**Mark 1:33** And all the city was gathered together at the door.

*Greek: ὅλη ἡ πόλις (F sg) → ἐπισυνηγμένη (F sg) "gathered"*
*Pattern: The city (feminine) → "gathered" (feminine participle) - city as collective*

### Revelation 11:8 (F → F Pattern - Strict Agreement)

**Revelation 11:8** And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.

*Greek: τῆς πόλεως τῆς μεγάλης (F sg) → ἥτις (F sg) "which"*
*Pattern: The city (feminine) → "which" (feminine relative) - city as entity*

**Isaiah 1:10** Hear the word of the LORD, ye rulers of Sodom; give ear unto the law of our God, ye people of Gomorrah.
