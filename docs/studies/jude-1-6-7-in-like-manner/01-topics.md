# Topics: Jude 1:6-7 "In Like Manner" Study

## Nave's Topical Dictionary Research

### SODOMITES
- The inhabitants of Sodom, many of whom openly practiced homosexuality
- Wickedness of: GEN 19:4-14
- Destroyed by fire as a judgment: GEN 19:24,25
- To be judged according to opportunity: MAT 11:24; LUK 10:12
- A proverbial term of reproach applied to those who practiced sodomy

### ANGEL (a spirit)
- A CELESTIAL SPIRIT
- Of different orders: ISA 6:2; 1TH 4:16; 1PE 3:22; Jude 1:9; REV 12:7
- Are obedient: PSA 103:20; MAT 6:10; LUK 11:2; 1PE 3:22; 2PE 2:11; **Jude 1:6**
- Are examples of meekness: 2PE 2:11; Jude 1:9
- Appearances to Lot in Sodom: GEN 19:1-17
