## PART B: Terms for God's Angels

### From Nave's: ANGEL (a spirit)
A celestial spirit, called:
- **ANGEL OF THE LORD** (malak YHWH) - Many OT appearances
- **ANGEL OF GOD** (malak elohim)
- **MORNING STARS** (Job 38:7)
- **HOSTS** (Gen 2:1; 32:2; Josh 5:14; Psa 103:21; Luke 2:13)
- **PRINCIPALITIES, POWERS** (Eph 3:10; Col 1:16)

Characteristics:
- Created (Gen 2:1; Neh 9:6; Col 1:16)
- Of different orders (Isa 6:2; 1 Thess 4:16; 1 Pet 3:22; Jude 1:9; Rev 12:7)
- Immortal (Luke 20:36)
- Do not marry (Matt 22:30; Mark 12:25; Luke 20:35)
- Holy (Matt 25:31; Mark 8:38)
- Elect (1 Tim 5:21)
- Innumerable (Deut 33:2; 2 Ki 6:17; Job 25:3; Psa 68:17; Heb 12:22; Jude 1:14)
- Mighty (Psa 103:20; 2 Pet 2:11)
- Wise (2 Sam 14:17,20)
- Men called angels (2 Sam 19:27)

### From Nave's: ARCHANGEL
- 1 Thess 4:16; Jude 1:9

### From Nave's: CHERUBIM
- Eastward of Eden (Gen 3:24)
- In the tabernacle (Exo 25:18-20; 37:7-9)
- Beneath the ark (1 Ki 8:6,7; 2 Ch 5:7,8; Heb 9:5)
- Figures on walls and curtains
- Symbolical (Ezek 1; 10)

### From Nave's: SERAPHIM
- Celestial beings (Isa 6:2,6)

### From Prior Study ([moses-angel-terminology](../moses-angel-terminology/CONCLUSION.md))
Moses's vocabulary for celestial beings:
- **Angel of the LORD** (malak YHWH) - 14 occurrences
- **Angel of God** (malak elohim) - 3 occurrences
- **Angels** (malakim) - 4 occurrences
- **An angel / his angel / mine angel** (malak) - 7 occurrences
- **Cherubim / Cherub** (keruvim / keruv) - 18 occurrences
- **Host** (tsaba) - 4 occurrences (celestial)

Key observation: Moses consistently uses "malak" (angel/messenger) for celestial beings. He does NOT use "sons of God" (bene elohim) for angels.

## Initial Observations

### Terms That Overlap (Potentially Ambiguous)
1. **Sons of God** (bene elohim/huioi theou) - Used for both?
2. **Holy ones** (qedoshim) - Used for both?
3. **Host** (tsaba) - Can refer to angels or armies

### Terms Unique to Humans
- Servant of the LORD (eved YHWH)
- My people (ammi)
- Israel, Hebrews, Israelites
- Church (ekklesia)
- Bride of Christ
- Adoption (huiothesia)

### Terms Unique to Angels
- Malak (angel/messenger)
- Cherubim
- Seraphim
- Archangel
- Principalities, Powers
- Morning stars (Job 38:7 context)
