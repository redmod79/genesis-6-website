# Word Studies

## Key Greek Term: Tartaroo (G5020)

### The Word

**Greek:** ταρταρόω (tartaroo)
**Strong's:** G5020
**Occurrence:** Only here in the entire New Testament
**Translation:** KJV renders "cast down to hell"

### What "Tartaroo" Meant

*Tartaroo* appears nowhere else in the entire Bible -- this is its sole occurrence. Peter chose this word deliberately. Understanding its cultural meaning is standard lexicography: the same principle that leads us to Greek lexicons for any NT word applies here.

In Greek cultural usage, Tartarus was where the Titans were imprisoned after **rebelling** against the divine order. The Titans did not marry humans; they waged war against Zeus. Peter's Greek-speaking audience would have understood *tartaroo* as punishment for **rebellion against divine authority** -- not sexual transgression.

This cultural background actually reinforces the biblical evidence. Peter was not endorsing Greek theology; he was selecting a word his audience would recognize as describing the punishment of rebels against divine authority -- which aligns precisely with Revelation 12's description of angelic rebellion.

**Key point:** While Greek background illuminates *why* Peter chose this word, we should also confirm the meaning from biblical parallels:

### Biblical Parallel: Revelation 12

While *tartaroo* is unique, the concept of angels being "cast down" is clearly described in Revelation 12:

| Revelation 12:9-10 | 2 Peter 2:4 |
|-------------------|-------------|
| "cast out" (ekballo, G1544) | "cast down" (tartaroo, G5020) |
| "cast down" (kataballo, G2598) | |

**The critical observation:** In Revelation 12, Satan and his angels are "cast out" and "cast down" - yet they remain **fully active** on earth:
- v.12: "the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath"
- v.13: "he persecuted the woman"
- v.17: "went to make war with the remnant"

### What "Tartaroo" Means (from Scripture)

Using Revelation 12 as the interpretive key:
- **Cast down from heaven** - expelled from their original position
- **Change in status** - "neither was their place found any more in heaven" (Rev 12:8)
- **NOT necessarily imprisonment** - Rev 12 shows cast-down angels who are active

## "Chains of Darkness" (seirais zophou)

### The Words

- **seira** (G4577) = chain, bond
- **zophos** (G2217) = darkness, gloom

### The Common Assumption

Most interpret this as literal imprisonment - angels physically bound and unable to act.

### The Problem

This assumption creates an unbiblical distinction:
- "Imprisoned angels" in 2 Peter 2:4
- "Active demons" elsewhere in Scripture

But Scripture doesn't support this distinction. Revelation 12 shows:
- Angels "cast down" → still active
- "Reserved unto judgment" → same as "knoweth he hath but a short time"

### Metaphorical Reading (Consistent with Rev 12)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chains** | Bound to judgment - fate sealed, cannot escape |
| **Darkness** | Spiritual state - separated from God's light |

This is similar to how humans can be described as being in "darkness" while still physically active:
- "the people which sat in darkness" (Matt 4:16)
- "men loved darkness rather than light" (John 3:19)
- "the power of darkness" (Luke 22:53, Col 1:13)

The angels are spiritually in darkness - condemned, separated from God - while still active.

## "Reserved unto Judgment" (eis krisin teroumenous)

### The Words

- **tereo** (G5083) = to keep, guard, reserve, watch over
- **krisis** (G2920) = judgment, decision

### The Parallel in Revelation 12

> "the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he **knoweth that he hath but a short time**" (Rev 12:12)

Both passages describe the same reality:
- Their fate is determined
- Judgment is coming
- They cannot escape
- But they remain active until that judgment

### Usage of "Reserved" Elsewhere

Peter uses the same word (tereo) in this context:

> "The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to **reserve** the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished" (2 Peter 2:9)

The "unjust" (human false teachers) are "reserved unto judgment" - yet they are clearly still active and causing harm. Being "reserved" doesn't mean imprisoned; it means their fate is sealed.

## Jude 6 Vocabulary

### "First Estate" (arche, G746)

**Meaning:** beginning, origin, principality, rule, domain

Jude says they "kept not their first estate" - they did not maintain their proper position/domain.

**Connection to Rev 12:** "neither was their place found any more in heaven" (Rev 12:8)

They abandoned their proper heavenly position by rebelling with Satan.

### "Own Habitation" (oiketerion, G3613)

**Meaning:** dwelling place, habitation

Jude says they "left their own habitation" - they departed from their proper dwelling.

**Connection to Rev 12:** They were "cast out" of heaven to earth (Rev 12:9).

They left heaven - whether voluntarily (rebellion) or as consequence of judgment.

## Summary

### What Word Studies Confirm

1. **Tartaroo** = "cast down" - parallel language to Revelation 12
2. **Chains of darkness** = condemned spiritual state, not necessarily physical prison
3. **Reserved unto judgment** = fate sealed, same as "short time" in Rev 12:12
4. **Kept not first estate / left habitation** = rebelled, lost place in heaven

### What Word Studies Do NOT Support

1. A special class of "imprisoned" angels separate from active demons
2. Literal physical chains preventing activity
3. A sexual-sin reading of *tartaroo* -- the Greek cultural background points to rebellion, not sexual transgression

### The Consistent Picture

Both 2 Peter 2:4 and Revelation 12 describe the same reality:
- Angels rebelled with Satan
- They were cast out of heaven
- They exist in spiritual darkness (condemned, separated from God)
- They remain active but their judgment is certain
- They have "but a short time" / are "reserved unto judgment"
