# Topics Research: Jude 6-7

## Question

What do Jude 6-7 actually teach about the angels' sin? Specifically:
1. What does "kept not their first estate, left their own habitation" mean?
2. What does "in like manner" (ton homoion tropon) connect?
3. What is "strange flesh" (heteras sarkos)?
4. Does this passage support the Genesis 6 angel interpretation?

## The Passage

> **Jude 6:** And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.
> **Jude 7:** Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.

## Methodological Approach

Following proper hermeneutics:
1. Examine what the text ACTUALLY STATES
2. Use Scripture to interpret Scripture (especially Rev 12, per 2 Peter 2:4 study)
3. Analyze the Greek grammar carefully
4. Avoid importing extra-biblical sources (1 Enoch) as authoritative
5. Let context determine meaning

## Key Interpretive Parallel: Revelation 12

From the 2 Peter 2:4 study, we established that Revelation 12 describes fallen angels:
- "Cast out" of heaven (Rev 12:9)
- "Neither was their place found any more in heaven" (Rev 12:8)
- Yet remain ACTIVE on earth (Rev 12:12-17)

This provides a biblical framework for understanding Jude 6:
- "Kept not their first estate" → lost their position in heaven
- "Left their own habitation" → cast out of heaven to earth
- "Reserved... unto judgment" → "knoweth he hath but a short time"

## Questions to Resolve

### 1. What does "kept not their first estate" mean?
- Greek: arche (G746) = beginning, origin, principality, domain
- Does this refer to leaving angelic nature, or leaving position/rank?

### 2. What does "left their own habitation" mean?
- Greek: oiketerion (G3613) = dwelling place
- Only used twice: Jude 6 and 2 Cor 5:2 (heavenly body)
- Does this mean leaving heaven, or leaving angelic form?

### 3. What does "in like manner" connect?
- Greek: ton homoion tropon
- Does it connect Sodom's sin TO the angels' sin?
- Or connect the surrounding cities TO Sodom?
- Or describe the manner of judgment?

### 4. What is "strange flesh"?
- Greek: heteras sarkos = different/other flesh
- Sodom's sin was homosexuality (same sex) - how is that "different" flesh?
- Or does this refer to the angels in Genesis 19 (different species)?

## Previous Studies to Reference

### 2 Peter 2:4 Study - Key Findings
- "Cast down" and "chains of darkness" describe spiritual state, not physical prison
- Revelation 12 shows "cast down" angels who remain active
- No need for "two classes" of fallen angels theory
- Genesis 6 connection is not required - Rev 12 provides simpler explanation

### 1 Peter 3:19-20 Study - Key Findings
- "Spirits in prison" were HUMANS who rejected Noah's preaching
- Different vocabulary: "spirits" (pneuma) vs "angels" (aggelos)
- Should not be conflated with fallen angels passages

### Fate of the Wicked Study - Key Findings
- "Eternal fire" results in destruction, not eternal conscious torment
- Sodom's fire has ceased but the destruction is permanent
- Same fate awaits Satan and his angels
