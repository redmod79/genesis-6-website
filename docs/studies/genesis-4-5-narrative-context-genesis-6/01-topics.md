# Nave's Topical Research: Genesis 4-5 Narrative Context for Genesis 6:1-4

## Topics Searched

### 1. SETH
- The third son of Adam and Eve (Gen 4:25,26; 5:3,8; 1Ch 1:1; Luk 3:38)
- Called SHETH (1Ch 1:1)
- Key: Genesis 4:25-26 establishes Seth as the replacement for Abel and the origin of the godly line

### 2. CAIN
- Son of Adam (Gen 4:1)
- Jealousy and crime of (Gen 4:3-15; Heb 11:4; 1Jn 3:12; Jude 1:11)
- Sojourns in the land of Nod (Gen 4:16)
- Children and descendants of (Gen 4:17,18)
- City of Judah (Jos 15:57)

### 3. CHILDREN OF GOD
- See RIGHTEOUS (Nave's redirects this to the righteous category)
- This topical redirect is significant: Nave's associates "children of God" with the righteous, not with angels

### 4. GENEALOGY
- General scriptures: Num 1:18; 2Ch 12:15; Neh 7:5
- Of no spiritual significance: Mat 3:9; 1Ti 1:4; Tit 3:9
- **From Adam to Noah: Gen 4:16-22; 5; 1Ch 1:1-4; Luk 3:36-38**
- To Abraham: Gen 11:10-32

### 5. CAINAN
- Son of Enos (Gen 5:9-15; 1Ch 1:2; Luk 3:37)
- Part of the Sethite genealogy

### 6. TUBAL-CAIN
- The inventor of edge tools (Gen 4:22)
- Part of Cain's line; contributes to technological/cultural development apart from God

### 7. INCEST / INTERMARRIAGE
- Defined and forbidden: Lev 18:6-18; 20:11,12,17,19-21
- Intermarriage warning: Deu 7:3-4 -- "Neither shalt thou make marriages with them... For they will turn away thy son from following me"
- The pattern: intermarriage with the ungodly leads to apostasy

### 8. NAMES OF GOD
- Redirects to GOD, NAMES OF
- Relevant because Genesis 4:26 introduces "calling upon the name of the LORD (YHWH)"

### 9. WORD OF GOD
- Multiple cross-references about God's word and name being called upon

### 10. PATRIARCH
- Head of a family (Act 7:9)
- The patriarchal structure is what Genesis 5 traces

## Key Nave's Observations

1. **CHILDREN OF GOD = RIGHTEOUS**: Nave's equates "children of God" with the righteous, supporting the Sethite view
2. **GENEALOGY from Adam to Noah**: Nave's lists both Gen 4:16-22 (Cain's line) and Gen 5 (Seth's line), recognizing the two-line structure
3. **INTERMARRIAGE**: The Deuteronomy 7:3-4 intermarriage warning provides the theological framework -- marriage with the ungodly leads to apostasy
4. **CAIN's descendants**: Noted for violence (4:3-15) and cultural achievement apart from God (4:17-22)
5. **SETH's line**: Noted for worship -- "then began men to call upon the name of the LORD" (4:26)
