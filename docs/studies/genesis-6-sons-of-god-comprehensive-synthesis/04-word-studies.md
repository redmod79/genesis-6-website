# Word Studies: Genesis 6 "Sons of God" -- Final Comprehensive Synthesis

This document summarizes the key word study findings from all 27 prerequisite studies.

---

## Hebrew Terms

### 1. בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים (bene ha'elohim) -- "Sons of God"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H1121 (ben) + H430 (elohim) |
| OT Occurrences | Gen 6:2, 4; Job 1:6; 2:1 (with article); Job 38:7 (without article) |
| Referent in Job | Heavenly beings in God's throne-room council |
| Referent in Gen 6 | Disputed -- the subject of this entire research project |
| Key Finding | Moses never uses this phrase for angels anywhere in the Pentateuch; he consistently uses "malak" (H4397) for angels 28+ times |
| Source Studies | [moses-angel-terminology](../moses-angel-terminology/CONCLUSION.md), [gods-people-vs-angels-terminology](../gods-people-vs-angels-terminology/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 2. מַלְאָךְ (malak) -- "Angel / Messenger"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H4397 |
| Pentateuch Occurrences | 28+ times (Gen 16, 19, 22; Exo 3; Num 22; etc.) |
| Usage | Moses's consistent term for celestial beings |
| Key Finding | This is what Moses writes when he means "angel." In Gen 19:1, Moses writes "angels" (malakim). If he meant angels in Gen 6:2, he used entirely different vocabulary. |
| Source Study | [moses-angel-terminology](../moses-angel-terminology/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 3. בָּנִים (banim) -- "Children / Sons"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H1121 (plural) |
| Key Passage | Deut 14:1: "Ye are the banim of the LORD your God" |
| Usage | Moses applies this term to Israel as God's children |
| Key Finding | Moses does call humans "children of God" -- but using "banim la'YHWH" (different preposition) rather than "bene ha'elohim" (construct form) |
| Source Study | [moses-human-god-relationship-terms](../moses-human-god-relationship-terms/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 4. שָׁחַת (shachath) -- "Corrupt / Destroy"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H7843 |
| Gen 6:11-12 | "The earth was corrupt"; "all flesh had corrupted his way" |
| Parallel Uses | Exo 32:7 (golden calf); Deut 4:16 (idolatry); Deut 9:12 (golden calf); Deut 31:29 (future apostasy); Judg 2:19 (religious apostasy) |
| Key Finding | When this word describes people corrupting THEMSELVES, it ALWAYS refers to moral corruption. Zero instances of genetic or biological meaning. |
| Source Study | [all-flesh-corrupted](../all-flesh-corrupted/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 5. דֶּרֶךְ (derek) -- "Way / Manner of Life"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H1870 |
| Gen 6:12 | "All flesh had corrupted his derek" |
| Meaning | Manner of life, conduct, behavior |
| Key Finding | Never means genetics, DNA, or biology in any OT passage. The phrase "corrupt + derek" is always moral. |
| Source Study | [all-flesh-corrupted](../all-flesh-corrupted/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 6. תָּמִים (tamim) -- "Perfect / Complete / Blameless"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H8549 |
| Gen 6:9 | "Noah was a just man and tamim in his generations" |
| Parallel Uses | Gen 17:1 (Abraham: "walk before me and be tamim"); Deut 18:13 ("thou shalt be tamim with the LORD"); Ps 37:37 ("mark the tamim man") |
| Key Finding | Tamim means moral integrity in every parallel. It never means genetic purity. Noah was morally blameless, not genetically uncontaminated. |
| Source Study | [flood-judgment-severity](../flood-judgment-severity/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 7. חָמָס (chamas) -- "Violence / Wrong"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H2555 |
| Gen 6:11, 13 | "The earth was filled with chamas" |
| Meaning | Violence, wrong, injustice |
| Key Finding | Moral term describing behavior. The violence trajectory traces from Cain (4:8) through Lamech (4:23-24) to universal corruption (6:11). |
| Source Study | [flood-judgment-severity](../flood-judgment-severity/CONCLUSION.md), [genesis-4-5-narrative-context-genesis-6](../genesis-4-5-narrative-context-genesis-6/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 8. נְפִלִים (nephilim) -- "Giants / Fallen Ones / Tyrants"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H5303 |
| Occurrences | Gen 6:4; Num 13:33 |
| Root | naphal (H5307) = "to fall" |
| Post-flood Use | Applied to Anakim (Num 13:33) who have traceable human genealogy (Arba > Anak > three sons, Josh 15:13-14) |
| Key Finding | The word describes what they WERE (giants/tyrants), not their origin. Post-flood application to humans with known human ancestry proves this. |
| Source Study | [nephilim-origin](../nephilim-origin/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 9. לָקַח (laqach) -- "To Take"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H3947 |
| Gen 6:2 | "They took [laqach] them wives" |
| Deut 7:3 | "His daughter shalt thou not take [laqach] unto thy son" |
| Key Finding | Standard OT marriage vocabulary. Same verb connects Gen 6:2 and Deut 7:3, linking the passages linguistically as describing the same kind of act: human intermarriage. |
| Source Study | [pentateuch-sexual-legislation-angel-unions](../pentateuch-sexual-legislation-angel-unions/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 10. תֶּבֶל (tebel) -- "Confusion / Perversion"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H8397 |
| Lev 18:23; 20:12 | Used specifically for unnatural sexual mixing (bestiality) |
| Key Finding | Moses had the vocabulary for cross-category sexual violations. He applied it to bestiality but never to angel-human contact -- suggesting he did not understand Gen 6 as describing such contact. |
| Source Study | [pentateuch-sexual-legislation-angel-unions](../pentateuch-sexual-legislation-angel-unions/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 11. אֱלֹהִים (elohim) -- "God / gods / judges"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | H430 |
| OT Occurrences | 2,685 |
| Semantic Range | God (~87.5%), false gods (~5.2%), human judges (Exo 21:6; 22:8-9, 28 -- 5 occurrences), superlative (Gen 23:6) |
| Key Finding | Jesus interprets "elohim" in Psalm 82 as referring to humans who received God's word (John 10:34-35). "Sons of elohim" is therefore not limited to "sons of divine beings." |
| Source Study | [psalm-82-gods](../psalm-82-gods/CONCLUSION.md), [second-temple-literature-genesis-6](../second-temple-literature-genesis-6/CONCLUSION.md) |

## Greek Terms

### 12. υἱός (huios) -- "Son"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G5207 |
| NT Occurrences | 382 |
| "Sons of God" | Rom 8:14, 19; 9:26; Gal 3:26; Matt 5:9; Luke 20:36 |
| Key Finding | Translation equivalent of Hebrew ben. All NT "sons of God" (huioi theou) occurrences refer to human believers. The NT NEVER calls angels "sons of God." |
| Source Study | [hebrew-greek-sons-of-god-comparison](../hebrew-greek-sons-of-god-comparison/CONCLUSION.md), [nt-sons-of-god-humans](../nt-sons-of-god-humans/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 13. τέκνον (teknon) -- "Child"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G5043 |
| "Children of God" | John 1:12; 11:52; Rom 8:16, 21; 9:8; Phil 2:15; 1 John 3:1, 2, 10; 5:2 |
| Key Finding | Emphasizes origin/birth/nature (vs. huios which emphasizes status). Used interchangeably with huioi in Rom 8 (huioi v.14; tekna v.16-17). All occurrences refer to humans. |
| Source Study | [nt-sons-of-god-humans](../nt-sons-of-god-humans/CONCLUSION.md), [gods-people-vs-angels-terminology](../gods-people-vs-angels-terminology/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 14. ἰσάγγελοι (isangeloi) -- "Equal to Angels"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G2465 |
| Occurrence | Luke 20:36 only (hapax legomenon) |
| Compound | isos (equal) + angelos (angel) |
| Key Finding | Predicate adjective with NO "in heaven" qualifier. Resurrected saints are isangeloi, which grounds both their immortality and their non-marriage in angelic NATURE. This removes the "in heaven" limitation that the angel view relies on. |
| Source Study | [jesus-angels-marriage-hermeneutical-ceiling](../jesus-angels-marriage-hermeneutical-ceiling/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 15. γαμέω / γαμίζω (gameo / gamizo) -- "To Marry / To Give in Marriage"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G1060 / G1061 |
| Key Passage | Matt 22:30; Mark 12:25; Luke 20:35 |
| Usage | gameo = active (groom's action); gamizo = passive (father giving bride) |
| Key Finding | Jesus uses BOTH terms, covering the entire marriage institution. "Neither marry nor are given in marriage" is comprehensive -- not about one aspect of marriage but the institution itself. |
| Source Study | [jesus-angels-marriage-hermeneutical-ceiling](../jesus-angels-marriage-hermeneutical-ceiling/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 16. ἄγγελος (angelos) -- "Angel / Messenger"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G32 |
| LXX Usage | Used for Job 1:6; 2:1; 38:7 -- but NOT for Gen 6:2, 4 |
| Key Finding | The LXX translators chose angeloi for Job (heavenly beings) but huioi for Gen 6 (sons). The same translators, handling the same Hebrew phrase, made a contextual distinction. |
| Source Study | [septuagint-genesis-6-translation](../septuagint-genesis-6-translation/CONCLUSION.md), [genesis-6-lxx-nt-comparison](../genesis-6-lxx-nt-comparison/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 17. πνεῦμα (pneuma) -- "Spirit"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G4151 |
| Heb 1:7, 14 | "Who maketh his angels spirits... ministering spirits" |
| Key Finding | Angels are spirits by nature. Temporary physical manifestation for a mission does not change essential nature or confer reproductive biology. |
| Source Study | [angels-physical-form](../angels-physical-form/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 18. σαρκὸς ἑτέρας (sarkos heteras) -- "Strange / Different Flesh"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G4561 (sarx) + G2087 (heteros) |
| Occurrence | Jude 1:7 |
| heteros meaning | "Different in kind" (qualitatively different) |
| Key Finding | Two viable readings: (1) homosexuality (men pursuing men = flesh of a different kind from the natural counterpart), supported by Gen 19:8-9, Ezek 16:50, Judg 19 parallel; (2) both homosexual intent and angelic reality (the flesh was actually angelic). The homosexual element is certain in either reading. |
| Source Study | [strange-flesh-jude-1-7](../strange-flesh-jude-1-7/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 19. ταρταρόω (tartaroo) -- "Cast to Tartarus"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G5020 |
| Occurrence | 2 Pet 2:4 only (hapax legomenon) |
| Key Finding | Describes God's judgment on rebelling angels. Rev 12 provides the biblical parallel (angels cast from heaven). "Cast down" angels remain active (Rev 12:12). Better interpreted through Rev 12 than through Greek mythology. |
| Source Study | [2-peter-2-4-angels-that-sinned](../2-peter-2-4-angels-that-sinned/CONCLUSION.md) |

### 20. ἀρχή (arche) -- "First Estate / Beginning / Principality"

| Field | Data |
|-------|------|
| Strong's | G746 |
| Jude 6 | "Angels which kept not their first estate [arche]" |
| Meaning | Position, rank, domain, principality |
| Key Finding | Describes the angels' POSITION, not their physical nature. "Kept not their arche" = abandoned their rank/domain. This is language of rebellion, not of taking human form or marrying. |
| Source Study | [jude-6-7-angels-sin](../jude-6-7-angels-sin/CONCLUSION.md) |
