# Topics: Genesis 6:4 Grammar Analysis

## Question
Does the Hebrew grammar of Genesis 6:4 support that the Nephilim existed BEFORE the sons of God married daughters of men?

---

## Research Focus

This study analyzes the Hebrew verb forms, clause structure, and word order of Genesis 6:4 to determine the temporal sequence of events.

### Key Grammatical Questions

1. What is the main clause of the verse?
2. What is the subordinate clause?
3. Do the verb tenses indicate the Nephilim existed prior to the unions?
4. What does the clause structure reveal about the relationship between the Nephilim and the "sons of God" unions?
