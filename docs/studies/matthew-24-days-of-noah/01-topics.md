# Topics Research

## Question
What did Jesus mean by "as the days of Noah" in Matthew 24:37? Was he referring to angels mixing with humans (Genesis 6 "sons of God"), or something else entirely?

## Nave's Topics Referenced

### ANTEDILUVIANS
Nave's specifically connects Matthew 24:37-39 and Luke 17:26,27 to the antediluvian (pre-flood) period:

```
-Wickedness of GEN 6:5-7
-Destruction of GEN 7:1,21-23; JOB 22:15-17; MAT 24:37-39; LUK 17:26,27; 2PE 2:5
```

Key observation: Nave's connects Jesus's reference to the **destruction** of the antediluvians, not to the "sons of God" passage specifically.

### NOAH
```
-His life preserved at the time of the great flood GEN 6:18; 7:8; MAT 24:38; HEB 11:7; 1PE 3:20
```

### FLOOD
The flood narrative is the primary context for Jesus's reference.

## The Core Issue

When Jesus said "as the days of Noah," what did HE choose to emphasize?

**Option A:** The supernatural element - angel-human mixing, Nephilim, corruption of bloodlines
**Option B:** The ordinary element - normal life activities, unawareness, sudden judgment

**This study will examine Jesus's own words to determine His intended meaning.**

## Preliminary Observations

Looking at the texts:

**Matthew 24:38** - Jesus emphasizes:
- "eating and drinking"
- "marrying and giving in marriage"
- "until the day that Noe entered into the ark"
- "knew not"

**Luke 17:27-28** - Jesus emphasizes:
- "They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage"
- Then parallels with Lot: "they did eat, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded"

**Notable omissions from Jesus's description:**
- No mention of "sons of God"
- No mention of "daughters of men"
- No mention of Nephilim/giants
- No mention of angels
- No mention of supernatural mixing
- No mention of corruption of flesh

Jesus chose to emphasize **ordinary activities** and **unawareness**, not supernatural corruption.
