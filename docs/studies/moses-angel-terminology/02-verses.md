# Verses: Moses's Terminology for Angels in the Pentateuch

## Category 1: "Angel of the LORD" (malak YHWH)

### Genesis

> **Gen 16:7** And the **angel of the LORD** found her by a fountain of water in the wilderness...
> **Gen 16:9** And the **angel of the LORD** said unto her, Return to thy mistress...
> **Gen 16:10** And the **angel of the LORD** said unto her, I will multiply thy seed...
> **Gen 16:11** And the **angel of the LORD** said unto her, Behold, thou [art] with child...

> **Gen 22:11** And the **angel of the LORD** called unto him out of heaven, and said, Abraham, Abraham...
> **Gen 22:15** And the **angel of the LORD** called unto Abraham out of heaven the second time...

### Exodus

> **Exo 3:2** And the **angel of the LORD** appeared unto him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush...

### Numbers

> **Num 22:22** And God's anger was kindled because he went: and the **angel of the LORD** stood in the way for an adversary against him...
> **Num 22:23** And the ass saw the **angel of the LORD** standing in the way, and his sword drawn in his hand...
> **Num 22:24** But the **angel of the LORD** stood in a path of the vineyards...
> **Num 22:25** And when the ass saw the **angel of the LORD**, she thrust herself unto the wall...
> **Num 22:26** And the **angel of the LORD** went further, and stood in a narrow place...
> **Num 22:27** And when the ass saw the **angel of the LORD**, she fell down under Balaam...
> **Num 22:31** Then the LORD opened the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the **angel of the LORD** standing in the way...
> **Num 22:32** And the **angel of the LORD** said unto him, Wherefore hast thou smitten thine ass these three times?
> **Num 22:34** And Balaam said unto the **angel of the LORD**, I have sinned...
> **Num 22:35** And the **angel of the LORD** said unto Balaam, Go with the men...

> **Exo 14:19** And the **angel of God**, which went before the camp of Israel, removed and went behind them...

## Category 3: "Angels" (plural - malakim)

> **Gen 19:1** And there came two **angels** to Sodom at even; and Lot sat in the gate of Sodom...
> **Gen 19:15** And when the morning arose, then the **angels** hastened Lot, saying, Arise, take thy wife...

> **Gen 28:12** And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the **angels of God** ascending and descending on it.

> **Gen 32:1** And Jacob went on his way, and the **angels of God** met him.

## Category 4: "His Angel" / "Mine Angel" / "An Angel"

> **Gen 24:7** The LORD God of heaven... he shall send **his angel** before thee, and thou shalt take a wife unto my son from thence.

> **Gen 24:40** And he said unto me, The LORD, before whom I walk, will send **his angel** with thee...

> **Gen 48:16** The **Angel** which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads...

> **Exo 23:20** Behold, I send **an Angel** before thee, to keep thee in the way...
> **Exo 23:23** For **mine Angel** shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites...

> **Exo 32:34** Therefore now go, lead the people unto [the place] of which I have spoken unto thee: behold, **mine Angel** shall go before thee...

> **Exo 33:2** And I will send **an angel** before thee; and I will drive out the Canaanite...

> **Num 20:16** And when we cried unto the LORD, he heard our voice, and sent **an angel**, and hath brought us forth out of Egypt...

## Category 5: "Cherubim" / "Cherub"

> **Gen 3:24** So he drove out the man; and he placed at the east of the garden of Eden **Cherubims**, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life.

> **Exo 25:18** And thou shalt make two **cherubims** [of] gold, [of] beaten work shalt thou make them, in the two ends of the mercy seat.
> **Exo 25:19** And make one **cherub** on the one end, and the other **cherub** on the other end...
> **Exo 25:20** And the **cherubims** shall stretch forth [their] wings on high, covering the mercy seat...
> **Exo 25:22** ...from between the two **cherubims** which [are] upon the ark of the testimony...

> **Exo 26:1** Moreover thou shalt make the tabernacle [with] ten curtains... with **cherubims** of cunning work shalt thou make them.
> **Exo 26:31** And thou shalt make a vail... with **cherubims** shall it be made.

> **Exo 36:8** ...made ten curtains... with **cherubims** of cunning work made he them.
> **Exo 36:35** And he made a vail... with **cherubims** made he it of cunning work.

> **Exo 37:7** And he made two **cherubims** [of] gold, beaten out of one piece...
> **Exo 37:8** One **cherub** on the end on this side, and another **cherub** on the [other] end...
> **Exo 37:9** And the **cherubims** spread out [their] wings on high...

## Category 6: "Host" (in celestial context)

> **Gen 2:1** Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the **host of them**.

> **Gen 32:2** And when Jacob saw them, he said, This [is] **God's host**: and he called the name of that place Mahanaim.

> **Deu 4:19** And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven, and when thou seest the sun, and the moon, and the stars, [even] all the **host of heaven**, shouldest be driven to worship them...

> **Deu 17:3** And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or moon, or any of the **host of heaven**, which I have not commanded...

---

## Category 7: What Moses Does NOT Use

### Terms NOT used by Moses for celestial beings in the Pentateuch:

- **"Sons of God"** (bene elohim) - NOT used for angels
- **"Morning stars"** - NOT used (appears in Job 38:7)
- **"Holy ones"** - NOT used for angels
- **"Watchers"** - NOT used (appears in Daniel)
- **"Spirits"** - NOT used for angels

---

## Summary: Moses's Vocabulary for Celestial Beings

| Term | Hebrew | Occurrences |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Angel of the LORD | malak YHWH | 14 times |
| Angel of God | malak elohim | 3 times |
| Angels (plural) | malakim | 4 times |
| His/mine/an angel | malak | 7 times |
| Cherubim/cherub | keruvim/keruv | 18 times |
| Host (celestial) | tsaba | 4 times |

**Total: Moses consistently uses "angel" (malak) or "cherubim" (keruv) when referring to celestial beings.**
