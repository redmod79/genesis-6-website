# Topics Research: Moses's Terminology for Angels

## Question

How does Moses refer to angels (celestial beings) in the Pentateuch (Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy)?

## Purpose

To document what terminology Moses actually uses when referring to celestial beings - without making assumptions or comparisons.

## Nave's Topical Bible Findings

### ANGEL Topic - Key Pentateuch References

**"Angel of the LORD" / "Angel of God":**
- GEN 16:7,9; 22:11; EXO 3:2; NUM 22:23,25,27,32,35

**"Angels of God" (plural):**
- GEN 28:12; 32:1

**"Hosts" (celestial):**
- GEN 2:1; 32:2

### CHERUBIM Topic - Pentateuch References

- GEN 3:24 - guarding Eden
- EXO 25:18-20; 37:7-9 - on the ark
- EXO 26:1,31; 36:8,35 - on tabernacle curtains

## Methodology

1. Search all occurrences of "angel/angels" in the Pentateuch
2. Search all occurrences of "cherub/cherubim"
3. Search all occurrences of "host" (celestial context)
4. Document ONLY what Moses wrote - no interpretation
