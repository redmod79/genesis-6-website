## Key Observations from Nave's

1. Nave's connects Sodom's sin explicitly with **homosexuality** ("many of whom openly practiced homosexuality")

2. The term "sodomite" (H6945 - qadesh) refers to **male cult prostitutes** in Israelite history

3. Nave's groups **sodomy** and **bestiality** together under similar scriptural categories (both in Lev 18:22-23; 20:13-16)

4. Both sins involve sexual acts outside the created male-female design
