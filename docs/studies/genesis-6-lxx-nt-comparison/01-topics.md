# Topics Research

## Question
Does the NT usage of "sons of God" (applied to humans) share the same Greek terminology as the LXX of Genesis 6? If so, does this linguistic overlap allow the Genesis 6 "sons of God" to refer to humans, assuming context permits?

## Baseline Study
This study builds on: [nt-sons-of-god-humans](../nt-sons-of-god-humans/CONCLUSION.md)

Key findings from that study:
1. NT clearly applies "sons of God" (huioi theou) to human believers
2. Believers ARE sons of God NOW on earth (Rom 8:14; 1 John 3:2; Phil 2:15)
3. Full manifestation awaits resurrection
4. Greek term used: οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ (hoi huioi tou theou)

## Related Previous Study
[septuagint-genesis-6-translation](../septuagint-genesis-6-translation/CONCLUSION.md)

Key findings from that study:
1. Standard LXX Genesis 6:2, 4 uses "sons of God" (υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ), NOT "angels"
2. LXX translators distinguished Genesis 6 from Job (where they used "angels")
3. The "angels" reading is a late variant (Codex Alexandrinus, 5th century)

## Research Question Refined
**If the LXX uses οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ in Genesis 6:2, and the NT uses the exact same phrase for human believers, does this establish that the phrase CAN refer to humans linguistically?**

## Nave's Topics Referenced

### ADOPTION (from previous study)
The ADOPTION topic lists Genesis 6 context alongside NT "sons of God" passages under "SPIRITUAL" adoption:
- NT passages: MAT 5:9,45; JHN 1:12,13; ROM 8:14-19,21,29; GAL 3:26,29; 4:5-7; PHP 2:15; 1JN 3:1,2,10; REV 21:7

### Key Observation
Nave's treats "sons of God" as a unified concept across Scripture, placing NT human-application passages in the same category. This suggests the phrase has semantic range that includes humans.

## Comparison Framework

### LXX Genesis 6:2, 4
- Greek: οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ θεοῦ
- Transliteration: hoi huioi tou theou
- Literal: "the sons of the God"

### NT Human Application Passages
| Passage | Greek | English |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Romans 8:14 | υἱοὶ θεοῦ | sons of God |
| Romans 8:19 | τῶν υἱῶν τοῦ θεοῦ | of the sons of God |
| Galatians 3:26 | υἱοὶ θεοῦ | sons of God |
| 1 John 3:1, 2 | τέκνα θεοῦ | children of God |

### Question to Resolve
Is the phrase in LXX Genesis 6 **linguistically identical** to the NT usage for humans?
If yes, then the linguistic range of "sons of God" clearly CAN include humans.
